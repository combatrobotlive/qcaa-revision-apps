    Unit 3 assessment support

    Use the current syllabus conditions as the authority for an assessment instrument. This independent practice set focuses on analysing and interpreting agricultural production evidence.

    Pasture and liveweight data

    Supplement levelAverage daily gainFeed conversion ratio
    Low0.42 kg/day8.1
    Medium0.57 kg/day6.9
    High0.59 kg/day7.8
    1. Describe the relationship between supplement level and average daily gain. (2)
    2. Compare feed conversion ratios and identify the most feed-efficient treatment. (2)
    3. Interpret the evidence to recommend a level, including one limitation. (4)
    Indicative response

    Gain increases strongly from low to medium then only slightly at high. Medium has the lowest FCR (6.9), therefore the best feed efficiency. Recommend medium subject to cost, animal health, pasture availability and replicates/statistical variation.

    Crop disease monitoring

    Management approachPlants with symptomsMarketable yield
    Calendar spray12%7.1 t/ha
    Monitoring + IPM14%7.0 t/ha
    No control31%5.4 t/ha
    1. Identify one trend in the data. (2)
    2. Analyse the production consequence of no control. (2)
    3. Interpret whether monitoring + IPM is a defensible option. (4)
    Indicative response

    Both managed treatments greatly reduce symptoms and lift marketable yield compared with no control. Monitoring + IPM has yield close to calendar spraying while potentially reducing unnecessary chemical use. A conclusion needs cost, pesticide-use, resistance and multi-season evidence.
