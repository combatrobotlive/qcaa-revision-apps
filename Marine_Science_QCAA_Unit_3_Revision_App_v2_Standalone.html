UNIT 3 DATA PRACTICE

Short marine-data scenarios

All figures are simulated practice data. Use the prompts, then reveal an indicative answer.

Reef recovery

Live coral cover changes from 52% before a disturbance to 24% after, then 34% later.

Try: Calculate the initial relative decrease; describe the recovery.

Indicative answer

(52 − 24)/52 × 100 = 53.8% decrease. Cover later rises but remains below the original level.

Thermal stress

Simplified weekly HotSpot values are 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 and 0.5 °C.

Try: Sum qualifying values (≥1 °C) as simplified DHW.

Indicative answer

DHW = 1.0 + 1.5 + 2.0 = 4.5 °C-weeks.

Carbonate chemistry

[Ca²⁺] = 0.010 mol/L, [CO₃²⁻] = 5.0×10⁻⁵ mol/L and Ksp = 6.0×10⁻⁷.

Try: Calculate Ω and state the consequence of more dissolved CO₂.

Indicative answer

Ω = 0.833. Extra CO₂ increases H⁺, reducing free carbonate ions.

Reef rugosity

A chain follows 18 m of reef contour over a 10 m straight span.

Try: Calculate rugosity and relate it cautiously to diversity.

Indicative answer

18 / 10 = 1.80. Greater complexity can provide refuges and niches, but other factors may also affect diversity.
