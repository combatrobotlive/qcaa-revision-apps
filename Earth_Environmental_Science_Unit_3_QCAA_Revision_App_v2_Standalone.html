UNIT 3 REFERENCE

Unit 3 course guide

Assessment pathway

  • IA1 Data test — 10%: Unit 3 data; 5 minutes perusal and 60 minutes working.
  • IA2 Student experiment — 20%: modify a Unit 3 class experiment and present an individual response.
  • External assessment: Unit 3 is examined with Unit 4 in two written papers.

High-value habits

  • Use the property measured by a survey to justify why it indicates a possible resource.
  • Separate economic viability from environmental and social feasibility.
  • Use values and units when analysing a resource dataset, then limit conclusions to what the evidence supports.
