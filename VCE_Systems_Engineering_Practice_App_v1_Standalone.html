2026 SAMPLE STYLE

Unfamiliar systems

Expect engineering knowledge to be applied to unfamiliar integrated systems. The VCAA's 2026 sample material includes design, energy comparison, fault diagnosis, materials/environment selection and emerging-technology critique.

Different from QCAA Engineering: this subject is much more explicitly mechatronic. Mechanical, electrical/electronic and control systems are integrated throughout Units 3–4, while the SAT carries a very large share of the study score.

2026 VCE Systems Engineering guide

Units 3 and 4

Unit 3 — Ethical systems design: integrated and controlled system principles/design plus clean energy technologies.

Unit 4 — Systems production and innovative technologies: producing/evaluating integrated systems plus new and emerging technologies.

Systems engineering process

Investigating & defining
Problem, users, context, constraints, design brief, evaluation criteria.
Generating & designing
Research, modelling, circuits, mechanisms, options and preferred solution.
Planning & managing
Work plan, timeline, materials, tools, milestones, risk and OHS.
Producing & implementing
Fabrication, assembly, wiring, programming, documentation and modification.
Evaluating
Diagnostic testing, data, criteria, critique, improvements and reflection.

Assessment

  • Units 3–4 SAC: 20% of the study score.
  • School-assessed Task (Unit 3 Outcome 1 + Unit 4 Outcome 1): 50%.
  • End-of-year examination: 30%.
  • The 2026 timetable allocates 15 minutes reading + 90 minutes writing for Systems Engineering.
  • One scientific calculator is authorised.

Current VCAA sample-question signals

  • Design an integrated automated system and annotate mechanical/electrical components.
  • Discuss energy technologies using data and cradle-to-cradle analysis.
  • Diagnose a fault and identify a parameter that could be monitored.
  • Select materials/components for a harsh operating environment.
  • Critique a new/emerging technology and discuss future applications and impacts.

Safety reminder
