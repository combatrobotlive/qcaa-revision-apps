Short marine-data scenarios

All figures are simulated practice data. Use the prompts, then reveal an indicative answer.

Biodiversity index

A quadrat contains four species with counts 8, 6, 4 and 2.

Try: Calculate Simpson’s diversity index.

Indicative answer

N = 20; Σn(n−1) = 100; SDI = 1 − 100/380 = 0.737.

Population dynamics

A fish population starts at 500. Births = 80, immigration = 30, deaths = 45, emigration = 20.

Try: Calculate the final population using BIDE.

Indicative answer

500 + 80 + 30 − 45 − 20 = 545 organisms.

Transect comparison

Equal sampled areas have 28 and 18 organisms respectively.

Try: Compare density and give one sampling limitation.

Indicative answer

The first area has the higher observed density. Habitat variation, detection or low replication may limit the comparison.
