Quick reference

Core calculations

Population density = population ÷ area

% change = (new − original) ÷ original × 100

Natural change = birth rate − death rate (when both use the same per-1000 basis)

17 per 1000 = 1.7%.

Spatial vocabulary

clustered • dispersed • linear • concentrated • gradient • core–periphery • corridor • anomaly/exception

Models

DTM: changing birth/death rates and natural growth through broad transition stages.

Rostow: linear stages of economic growth; useful but contested.

Wallerstein: core / semi-periphery / periphery relationships.
