Terminology and response craft

QCAA syllabus

Interpret a source

  • Identify a precise source feature.
  • Explain the meaning you draw from it.
  • Link that meaning to the question and context.
  • Keep evidence and inference distinct.

Analyse perspective

  • Identify viewpoint, purpose or assumptions.
  • Explain how these shape representation or action.
  • Use the relevant historical, social and cultural context.
  • Do not just label a source “biased”.

Evaluate significance

  • Make a clear, qualified judgment.
  • Use criteria such as scope, depth, duration and consequence.
  • Weigh evidence and a relevant limitation or alternative perspective.
  • Return to the question wording.

Use respectful language

  • Be specific about peoples, communities and locations.
  • Use locally preferred terms where known.
  • Avoid deficit framing and generalisations.
  • Respect cultural authority, permissions and restricted knowledge.
