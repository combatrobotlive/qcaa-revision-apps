Core ecology calculations

  • Lincoln index: N = (M × n) / m
  • Species richness, relative abundance, % cover, % frequency
  • Simpson diversity index
  • Population change from births, deaths, immigration and emigration
  • Ecological/biomass transfer efficiency
  • Gross vs net productivity

Assessment structure

  • IA1 — 10%: Unit 3 Data Test, 5 min perusal + 60 min working, 2–4 unseen datasets.
  • IA2 — 20%: Unit 3 Student Experiment.
  • IA3 — 20%: Unit 4 Research Investigation.
