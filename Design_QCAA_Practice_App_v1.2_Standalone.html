Practice the task, not trivia

The external assessment asks students to respond to an unseen Unit 4 stimulus using the develop phase: devise a range of ideas, evaluate and refine them against the supplied criteria, then synthesise a sustainable design concept. This app generates fresh design briefs and gives you a studio to practise that process.

What strong responses visibly do

✓ Diverge. Show multiple genuinely different ways of responding, not one idea redrawn four times.
✓ Use the stimulus. Make details in the brief visible in the ideas, not just in annotations.
✓ Evaluate and change. Identify strengths, limitations and implications against every criterion, then make purposeful refinements.
✓ Synthesise. The final concept should combine valuable attributes from multiple ideas and sustainability information.

2026 focus

The revised syllabus uses plainer language and updates the design lifecycle and circular-design content. The app therefore rotates strategies such as product life extension, closed-loop/take-back, modularity, product-as-a-service, embedding intelligence and smart material choices.

Important: this app supports practice and self-reflection; it does not pretend to automatically mark the quality of a student's sketches.
