Section timers below are optional study budgets only. The actual examination has one shared 120-minute working period, not separately timed sections.

Full simulation has no pause. The timer continues while the tab is closed or in the background and resumes from the saved deadline. Answers and planning are locked during perusal; answer guides appear only after the attempt ends. Opening a blank paper before starting means it is no longer unseen to you.

How to use this mode

Choose an unused paper, close your notes and start the full simulation. Read during perusal, then answer all questions during working time. Use the numbered buttons to move around the paper and flag questions to revisit.

After submission, multiple choice is checked locally. For written responses, compare your work with the indicative guidance and enter your own marks against each criterion. Those marks are not an official grade.

Written exam, not an IA3 folio

Use the data supplied in the paper to explain relationships, appraise a strategy and justify decisions. Do not import a personal folio or rely only on the physical activity studied in class. All sporting contexts are explained in the stimulus.

Focused modes use suggested practice timings, not separate QCAA exam conditions. The printed papers support handwritten practice. After an attempt ends, the optional AI export prepares a marking-advice prompt for you to copy or download. There is no in-app AI marking or writing generator.

Exam attempts on this browser

Exam attempts use their own subject-and-unit storage key. Normal Unit 4 revision progress is unchanged. Storage belongs to this browser and site; moving devices or using private browsing can lose it. Back up important work.
