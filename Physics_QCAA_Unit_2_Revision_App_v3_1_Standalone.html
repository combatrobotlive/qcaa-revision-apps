Physics Formula and Data Guide

Unit 2 focus, mapped to the current QCAA Physics 2025 formula and data book. Unit 2 assessment is school-developed, so permitted reference materials depend on your school’s conditions.

Explained entries
Authoritative sources: QCAA Physics 2025 v1.3 syllabus (January 2026) and QCAA Physics 2025 formula and data book. Entries labelled Official book preserve the printed notation. Rearrangements, explanations, worked examples and entries labelled teaching-only are annotations for learning and are not part of the unannotated QCAA formula/data book. Find it on the official book

Select the printed page number shown at the bottom of the QCAA book. Unit 2's main printed formula sections are on pages 3 and 4; processing-of-data formulas are on page 1, with useful constants on pages 5 and 6.

Page numbering: this map uses the book's printed page labels 1 of 8 through 8 of 8, not the PDF viewer's cover-page index.

Which formula?

Choose the relationship that best matches the quantities and task before doing any calculation.

Symbols, units and conversions

SI conversion routine
  1. Write the given value and prefix.
  2. Convert to the base SI unit before substitution.
  3. Keep powers of ten visible until the final step.
  4. Check the final unit and sensible magnitude.
Common Unit 2 traps
  • Use signed displacement/velocity consistently; speed and distance are scalars.
  • Graph gradients and areas depend on the axis quantities and units.
  • Refraction angles are measured from the normal, not the surface.
  • For standing waves, identify open/closed boundary conditions before selecting a formula.

Guide scope and source notes

The official formula/data book contains content from the whole Physics course. This Unit 2 app foregrounds Processing of data, Linear motion and force, and Waves. Other pages remain visible in the sheet map so students learn the real book layout without implying that every printed formula belongs to Unit 2.

The impulse/force–time connection and intensity–amplitude relationship are clearly labelled as syllabus/teaching relationships when they are not separately printed as formulas in the QCAA book.
