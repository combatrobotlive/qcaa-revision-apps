Assessment support

Units 1 and 2 use school-developed assessment. Use this app to rehearse civil-law analysis, legal terminology and evidence-based evaluation in the format your teacher specifies.

Use your teacher’s current task sheet and stimulus material for any assessed work. This app provides revision prompts and does not supply assessment responses.
