The biggest difference from the other state apps: there is no fixed statewide Engineering Studies examination. ACT BSSS uses school-based curriculum and assessment. For a standard 1.0 unit, students complete 3–5 assessment tasks; in a T course, Design Process and Design Solution(s) must each contribute 40–60% of the unit assessment. A-course ranges are 30–70% each.
SYSTEMS

Broad engineering contexts

The course can use building, mechanical, electrical or mechatronic systems. The syllabus examples include levers, mechanisms, gears, structures/load analysis, control programming, hydraulics, renewable energy, aerodynamics and circuit design.

DESIGN

Evidence beats a finished object

Students investigate needs, develop briefs, choose materials/components, model and prototype, test against requirements, iterate and justify decisions using evidence.

T COURSE

Higher cognitive demand

Year 12 T-course standards emphasise critical analysis, synthesis, evaluation of validity/reliability, solving complex problems, innovative high-quality solutions and logically justified decisions.

LOCAL FLEXIBILITY

There is no one ACT formula list

Colleges must teach all content descriptions but may choose the engineering contexts used to demonstrate them and may add content that suits students and local programs. Numerical questions in this app are therefore labelled as common engineering practice contexts, not a mandated BSSS equation list.

COURSE GUIDE

What ACT Engineering Studies actually assesses

Assessment criteria

BSSS identifies two overarching assessment criteria: knowledge and understanding and skills. Colleges construct task-specific rubrics or marking schemes; the course achievement standards inform those judgments but are not themselves a rubric for a single task.

Task types

Design Process examples include design development/documentation, reports, research, portfolios, project management, reviews, short responses and presentations. Design Solution(s) examples include products, prototypes, digital artefacts, software, networks, websites and major projects.

Standard unit requirements

A 1.0 standard unit is generally 55 hours and must have 3–5 assessment tasks. A 0.5 unit is 27.5 hours with 2–3 tasks. For T courses, Design Process and Design Solution(s) each sit in the 40–60% range; A courses use 30–70%.

Why this app looks different

There is no ACT Engineering Studies external exam specification to imitate. Instead, this app drills the common knowledge, design reasoning, calculations, project decisions and evaluation habits that can transfer across locally designed college programs.

Creator note: if an ACT teacher wanted to adopt this seriously, I would expect their local program to determine which physics/electronics/structures calculations deserve the biggest bank. The BSSS course deliberately leaves that context flexible.
