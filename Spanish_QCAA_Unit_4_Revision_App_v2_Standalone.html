Assessment support

IA3 and external assessment support

Unit 4 includes a multimodal internal assessment and the external assessment. Practice here is original and designed around the published task demands, not copied QCAA material.

Assessment support

IA3 · Multimodal presentation and interview · 30%

Synthesise three Spanish stimuli related to this unit, include multiple perspectives including your own, deliver a structured multimodal response in Spanish, then respond orally to unseen reflective questions. The interview is unprepared and may run up to 7 minutes.

Assessment support

External assessment · Combination response · 25%

Prepare for short responses in English or Spanish and extended Spanish responses. The examination can use 3–6 unseen authentic Spanish stimuli (combined up to 1000 words) and includes written plus audio/audiovisual material.

Assessment support

EA response discipline

Select evidence before writing, interpret rather than merely translate, synthesise across stimuli when asked, and shape Spanish extended writing for purpose, audience and context.

Use: this section is revision support only. Follow your school’s task sheet and the current QCAA syllabus/assessment specifications for formal assessment.
Reference

Unit 4 language and response toolkit

Quick reminders for interpreting and creating Spanish in this unit.

Future and conditional

Use future for predictions/plans and conditional for hypothetical or tentative outcomes. Combine with si + imperfect subjunctive for unreal/hypothetical conditions.

Persuasive and evaluative connectors

por eso, por lo tanto, no obstante, a pesar de, en cambio, además, dado que, siempre que, aunque and en definitiva.

IA3 oral interaction

Answer the question first, then extend with a reason, example or consequence. Ask for repetition or clarification naturally if needed rather than freezing.

EA extended writing

Plan purpose + audience + register; integrate stimulus ideas without copying; use paragraph progression and varied language; leave time to check agreements, tense consistency and accents.
