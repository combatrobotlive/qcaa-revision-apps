ASSESSMENT TYPE 1
20%

Specialised Skills Task

Two specialised-skills tasks. Students demonstrate skills and knowledge needed for solution realisation, apply processes/techniques in the chosen context, evaluate their skill development and review how the technique may influence the final solution.

Official workshop guidance: up to 6 minutes or 1000 words.

ASSESSMENT TYPE 2
50%

Design Process & Solution

One substantial task or up to three smaller discrete tasks. Evidence can span investigation, design development, production and evaluation. The work must showcase and evaluate the solution/product.

Official workshop guidance: up to 12 minutes or 2000 words.

ASSESSMENT TYPE 3 · EXTERNAL
30%

Resource Study

Two parts: investigate/test two or more materials/components and show how the evidence affects selection; then investigate ethical, legal, economic and/or sustainability issues related to the solution.

Current guidance: written/multimodal or a combination; up to 2000 words or 12 minutes.

The biggest SA difference: there is no statewide Stage 2 engineering examination for this subject. The external component is the Resource Study, so success depends much more on evidence quality, testing, justified selection and analysis of impacts than on revising a prescribed formula list.

REFERENCE

SACE Stage 2 DTE guide

Learning requirements

  1. Investigate/analyse design features, processes, materials and production techniques and use creative thinking.
  2. Plan, develop and test concepts and communicate possible solutions.
  3. Apply skills, processes, engineering procedures and techniques to realise a solution.
  4. Evaluate against the design brief and reflect on development/realisation.
  5. Analyse ethical, legal, economic and/or sustainability issues associated with technology, materials, processes and/or solution design.

Assessment weighting

  • Specialised Skills Task — 20%
  • Design Process & Solution — 50%
  • Resource Study — 30% externally assessed

The SACE website describes the first two as school assessed/moderated and the Resource Study as externally assessed.

Resource Study — Part 1

Investigate and analyse the functional characteristics and properties of two or more materials/components being considered for the solution. Research and testing should affect the eventual selection.

Resource Study — Part 2

Investigate and analyse an ethical, legal, economic and/or sustainability issue related to the solution. Strong work connects the issue to the actual design/realisation decision rather than writing a generic technology essay.

Common technical calculations: this app includes a few context-appropriate calculations as skill practice, but SACE DTE does not publish a single statewide engineering formula syllabus comparable to QCAA Engineering or WA Engineering Studies. Schools choose the technological lens within the context.
