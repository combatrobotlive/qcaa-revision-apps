Elements of drama

Skills of drama

    Purpose, context and meaning

    • Purpose: identify the dramatic intent, not merely the topic.
    • Context: interpret the cultural, political, sociological, philosophical or other frame that is relevant to the text and performance.
    • Meaning: explain what the audience is invited to understand, feel, question or reconsider through the dramatic action.
    • Evidence: refer to a specific moment, convention, element, acting choice or stagecraft detail.

    Command words

    • Interpret: make meaning from text, purpose and context.
    • Manipulate: deliberately shape dramatic languages to create action and meaning.
    • Analyse: explain relationships among choices, action, audience and meaning.
    • Evaluate: judge effectiveness against purpose, context and meaning.
    • Justify: support a decision with relevant dramatic evidence and reasoning.
