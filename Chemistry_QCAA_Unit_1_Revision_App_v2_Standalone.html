UNIT 1 REFERENCE

Chemical fundamentals — structure, properties and reactions

Official topic headings are shown below. The bullet points are revision-coverage labels used by this app.

Properties and structure of atoms
  • Atomic number, mass number and subatomic particles
  • Isotopes and nuclide notation
  • Relative atomic mass from isotopic abundance
  • Electron configuration of main-group atoms and ions
  • Chromium and copper electron-configuration exceptions
  • Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion and Hund's rule
  • First ionisation energy periodic trends
  • Successive ionisation energies and valence electrons
  • Atomic radius periodic trends
  • Electronegativity periodic trends
  • Emission spectra and quantised energy levels
  • Atomic absorption spectroscopy
  • Mass spectrometry and isotope patterns
Properties and structure of materials
  • Ionic bonding and ionic-lattice properties
  • Metallic bonding and metal properties
  • Molecular covalent substances
  • Giant covalent network structures
  • Inferring structure from physical properties
  • Pure substances, mixtures and separation
Chemical reactions — reactants, products and energy change
  • Moles, mass and molar mass
  • Particles, moles and Avogadro's constant
  • Empirical formula from composition data
  • Limiting reagents, theoretical yield and percentage yield
