Three modes per family: A checks the core concept, B applies or deconstructs it, and C repairs a common misconception. Write before opening the indicative guide, then mark yourself honestly to make the progress filters useful.
Assessment focus: Students complete any two of the three syllabus topics. This app includes all three so a school can select the two it teaches. Unit 2 assessment is school-developed; use the conditions on your own instrument.
Syllabus alignment: QCAA Philosophy & Reason 2025 v1.3 (January 2026). The app paraphrases required subject matter and should be used with your school’s course and assessment instrument.
