Exam selection

The 2026 syllabus fixes two papers, 5 min perusal + 90 min working each. Exact 2026 raw marks/section counts are not published; this simulator uses the published 2025 55+55 mark shape as a labelled practice blueprint.

Permitted materials: QCAA-approved graphics or scientific calculator and the QCAA Chemistry formula and data book. During timed perusal, questions and options are visible but responses are locked.
