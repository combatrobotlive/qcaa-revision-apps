Symbols, units and relationships

Calculus and motion

SymbolMeaningCheck
x, tDisplacement/independent variable; timeUse a single unit system. t and period T are not interchangeable.
v, aVelocity and accelerationFor motion, use signed direction; a can be written as dv/dt, d²x/dt² or v dv/dx.
A, ω, T, fSHM amplitude, angular frequency, period, frequencyω has rad per time; T has time; f = 1/T.
V, r, hVolume, radius and heightFor related rates, differentiate the whole model with respect to time before substituting a momentary value.

Statistical inference

SymbolMeaningCheck
λPositive exponential rate parameterHas reciprocal-time units; 1/λ has time units.
μ, σ, sPopulation mean, population SD, sample SDUse σ for a known population SD and s in the printed confidence-interval formula.
X̄, nSample mean; sample sizeSD(X̄) = σ/√n, not σ/n.
z, ECritical value; margin of errorE has the same units as the measured variable; z is dimensionless.

Common relationship errors

Log integrals: retain the absolute value and the condition that the denominator is non-zero.

Simpson’s rule: n must be even; the 4, 2, 4, … weights apply only between the two endpoint values.

Volumes: square the radius/function before integrating, and report cubic units.

What the Formula Book does not do for you

It provides selected relationships; it does not choose substitution, perform a partial-fraction decomposition, separate a differential equation, set a direction convention or interpret a confidence interval. Those methods are explicitly labelled teaching relationship / method in this guide and remain subject knowledge.

Sources, scope and teaching additions

The official notation, section titles and PDF locations were checked against the QCAA Specialist Mathematics 2025 Formula book (June 2026 edition) and Specialist Mathematics 2025 v1.4 syllabus. The guide defaults to Unit 4 and only uses shared prerequisite entries when the Unit 4 syllabus specifically applies them (e.g. cone/sphere related rates).

Formula Book notation and page order are QCAA material. This navigation map, the explanations, conditions, rearrangements, examples and selection practice are independently prepared teaching material. They are not QCAA marking guidance and are not supplied as annotated material in an examination.
