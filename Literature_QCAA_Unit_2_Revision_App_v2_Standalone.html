Plan quality check

The focus is the exact question, not a memorised topic.
The claim makes an arguable interpretation or intentional creative choice.
Evidence/details are precise enough to analyse or craft with.
Each argument/design choice develops the claim rather than repeats it.
Writer’s choices and audience effects are connected to representations, values or ideas.

Text selection reminder

Revision-reference method

  1. Record exact, short evidence or details in your own notes.
  2. Link each one to a deliberate textual choice.
  3. Explain the interpretation or audience effect it supports.
