ASSESSMENT PREPARATION

Unit 4 assessment support

IA3 · Multimodal presentation and interview · 30%

IA3 relates to Unit 4 Topic 1 and/or Topic 2. Students synthesise ideas from three teacher-provided Italian stimulus texts, identify different perspectives including their own, create an individual multimodal response in Italian, then answer unseen open-ended reflective questions in spoken Italian.

The task uses one seen stimulus studied in class plus two unseen stimuli supplied with the task.

External assessment · Combination response · 25%

The QCAA external examination relates to Unit 4. It may require sentences or paragraphs in English (up to 100 words per question), sentences or paragraphs in Italian (up to 100 words per question), and extended responses in Italian (up to 400 words per question).

QCAA provides 3–6 authentic Italian stimuli, combined up to 1000 words, including at least one written and one audio/audiovisual text.

Combination-response habits

  • Answer the exact cognition and requested response language.
  • Use distinct, relevant evidence rather than copying large sections.
  • For PACT, infer meaning from evidence rather than naming a feature only.
  • For extended Italian, sustain a clear position, synthesis and appropriate register.

Interview habits

  • Prepare ideas, not memorised scripts.
  • Listen to the whole unseen question before responding.
  • Extend answers with reasons, examples and comparisons.
  • Practise repairing communication when a word is missing.
