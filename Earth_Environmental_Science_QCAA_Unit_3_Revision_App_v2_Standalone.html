IA1 data rehearsal

A settling test records mean turbidity of 120 NTU at 0 minutes and 30 NTU after 20 minutes.

  1. Calculate the percentage reduction in turbidity.
  2. Describe the relationship between settling time and turbidity.
  3. Explain why this result does not establish that the water is safe to drink.
Practice guidance

Reduction = (120 − 30) ÷ 120 × 100 = 75%. Turbidity decreases over the measured period. Settling can reduce suspended particles, but the data do not measure dissolved contaminants or microorganisms.

UNIT 3 REFERENCE

Key ideas and relationships

Revision habit: For a resource decision, distinguish evidence of a deposit from evidence that extraction is feasible. In data questions, use values and units to establish the pattern, then limit the conclusion to what the evidence supports.
