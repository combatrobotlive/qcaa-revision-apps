REFERENCE

Language and response toolkit

PACT

  • Purpose: what the text is trying to achieve.
  • Audience: who the text is aimed at.
  • Context: the situation in which the text exists.
  • Tone: the attitude or feeling communicated.

Evidence habit

  • Make a clear conclusion first.
  • Select distinct evidence that actually supports it.
  • Explain the link instead of copying long chunks.
  • For German responses, prioritise clear meaning, accurate word order and appropriate register.
