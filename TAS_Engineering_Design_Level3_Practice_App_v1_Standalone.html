REFERENCE

2026 Engineering Design Level 3 guide

Course identity

Course code: EDN315123 · Level: 3 · TCE credit points: 15 · current for 2026.

The course is classified as Professional Studies and is designed to replicate professional engineering processes: authentic briefs, research, ideation, prototyping, testing, stakeholder work, professional standards and communication.

External folio requirements

  • One folio assesses Criteria 1, 2, 4, 5 and 8.
  • Maximum 40 A4-equivalent pages.
  • Maximum 500 MB total.
  • Includes a 1500–2000 word research essay.
  • Current 2026 requirements accept a recognised referencing system rather than requiring one specific referencing system.
  • The final solution can be a product, service or environment and should be suitably resolved against the success criteria.

External engineering-report sequence

  1. Title page
  2. Design brief
  3. Research analysis
  4. Design development
  5. Production proposal
  6. Design production
  7. Final engineered design
  8. Evaluation and recommendations
  9. References

Important project advice

TASC explicitly advises students to control the scope of the extended project. The aim is a fully resolved engineering design project in which every stage of the engineering design process is properly addressed, not an expansive project that cannot be finished or evidenced well.

Numerical practice in this app

The course expects learners to use technological, scientific and mathematical concepts to interpret problems and support decisions, but it does not prescribe a statewide list of engineering formulas like some interstate engineering subjects. Any calculation examples here are therefore labelled as engineering-context practice, not as a TASC formula syllabus.
