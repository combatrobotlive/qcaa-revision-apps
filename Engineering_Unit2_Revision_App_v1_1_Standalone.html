Independent classroom revision

Formula reference

Average velocityv_av = s / ts is displacement. State a direction when relevant.
Average accelerationa = (v - u) / tUse a signed velocity change.
Mechanical advantageMA = load / effortBoth forces must use consistent units.
Velocity ratioVR = d_effort / d_loadUse consistent distance units.
Electrical relationshipsV = IR   P = VI   E = PtJoules require watts multiplied by seconds.
Two resistorsSeries: RT = R1 + R2
Parallel: 1/RT = 1/R1 + 1/R2Series current is common; parallel voltage is common.
Cu-Ni lever rulef_solid = (C0 - CL) / (CS - CL)
f_liquid = (CS - C0) / (CS - CL)CL and CS are phase compositions, not their fractions.
Inverse lever ruleC0 = f_liquid CL + f_solid CSFractions sum to 1; multiply by 100 for percentages.

Checking answers

Use one number per field. Examples: 2400 J, 2.4 kJ, 2.4e3 J, 2.4 x 10^3 J. A bare number uses the unit beside the field. Most numerical checks allow 1.5% rounding tolerance; whole-number counts and truth-table entries require exact values. A percentage field expects 70 for 70%, not 0.70.

Automatic marks check final answers, not handwritten working or official method marks. Written-response marks are explicitly self-assessed. Revealed solutions remain flagged as assisted for that attempt. A new seed starts a different attempt.

Exam-style practice

The 50-mark mock follows the structure used in your earlier practice papers: 30 core marks, 10 applied marks and a 10-mark integrated scenario. It contains 10 MC questions, 10 short core questions, two applied questions and one CU scenario. Answers are hidden until you finish the mock. Suggested timing: 10 minutes planning and 90 minutes working; the app does not enforce a timer. These are practice settings, not mandatory QCAA Unit 2 conditions.

Study and assessment boundaries

Hot/cold working, annealing, casting, forging, welding, rolling and extrusion remain in this Unit 2 bank. Dedicated destructive/non-destructive testing questions and Unit 4 steel-hardening/CCT questions are not included. Two-resistor circuits and basic motion are retained even in the integrated scenarios. Cu-Ni diagrams and material screening tables are labelled teaching data.

Offline use and sharing

The standalone HTML contains its own scripts and generates every diagram locally. The separate PWA folder can be hosted on HTTPS or localhost and cached for offline use after its first successful load. A file link is not a portable student link: use the exact set code, or a link to a hosted copy. Teacher Mode is a convenience tool, not a secure authentication boundary.
