Exam selection

The 2026 syllabus specifies two papers, each with 5 min perusal and 90 min working. The published 2025 paper shape was 20 MC + 24 short-response marks in Paper 1 and 46 short-response marks in Paper 2; this is a labelled practice model, not a prediction of 2026 raw marks.

Permitted materials: QCAA-approved graphics or scientific calculator. During timed perusal, questions and options are visible but response fields are locked.
