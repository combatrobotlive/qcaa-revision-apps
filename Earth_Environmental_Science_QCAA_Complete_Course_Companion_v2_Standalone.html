2026 Earth & Environmental Science EA guide

What can be externally assessed?

Unit 3 Topic 1: Use of non-renewable Earth resources.

Unit 3 Topic 2: Use of renewable Earth resources.

Unit 4 Topic 1: The cause and impact of Earth hazards.

Unit 4 Topic 2: The cause and impact of global climate change.

Current exam conditions

  • External assessment weighting: 50%.
  • Two written papers.
  • Paper 1: 5 min perusal + 90 min working.
  • Paper 2: 5 min perusal + 90 min working.
  • QCAA-approved graphics or scientific calculator permitted.
  • Questions may use multiple choice, single words, sentences/paragraphs, calculations and unseen graphs/tables/diagrams.
  • No extended-response section under the revised syllabus.

High-value habits

  • Read exactly what is being compared: with/without, Site A/Site B, or two different variables.
  • Use values, units and directions of change when analysing data.
  • For predictions, state the prediction and justify it with the specific stimulus evidence.
  • For remote sensing, know what the method actually measures and why that can reveal a resource.
  • For coefficients, identify what the coefficient represents before substituting numbers.
  • When interpreting climate or hazard datasets, distinguish pattern/association from certainty about causation.

IA2 checklist

Repeat-measurement helper

Enter at least two repeat readings for one condition, separated by commas or spaces. Half-range describes repeat spread; it is not automatically a confidence interval or the total measurement uncertainty.

Use the task’s marking guide

Organise evidence for forming the investigation, finding data, analysing data, and interpreting and evaluating your findings. Checking every box does not guarantee a mark.

IA3 · Research investigation · 20%

Evaluate a teacher-provided claim about Unit 4 hazards or climate change using secondary evidence.

IA3 evidence register

For each source record its author/date/link, relevant data, uncertainty, methods, geographic scope and limitations. Compare independent sources and investigate disagreement.
