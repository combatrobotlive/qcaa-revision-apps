Simulated Exam

Original source-based rehearsal for your selected external-assessment aspect.

Practice · not a QCAA paper

Set up your rehearsal

Three paragraph-response questions, six cited source digests and separate question/stimulus and response books. All sources belong to the selected aspect. Planning permits unmarked notes; response boxes unlock when working begins. Skip, flag and return using the exam sidebar. There is no pause; the clock continues if you reload or close the app. Submit or let time expire to unlock review and AI export.

The current syllabus specifies one written short-response external assessment on the selected Unit 4 topic/aspect, worth 25%. The full rehearsal uses its 15-minute planning and 120-minute working periods. July 2026 examples show three questions and six sources per topic; the syllabus allows evidence from up to 12 sources. Published examples use placeholder marks: this practice assigns no official question weights or total marks. Short and untimed modes are learning accommodations; starting work early is recorded as assisted. There are no separately timed official sections here.

Format, technology conditions and evidence limitations

The official written assessment is completed by hand. This app is a typed rehearsal aid; print its response book for handwriting practice. The August 2026 QCAA equipment list specifies no additional subject-specific equipment for Modern History. General permitted stationery applies: use black or blue non-erasable pen for written responses. Calculators, digital devices, notes and other prohibited materials are not part of the standard conditions; follow any approved access arrangements and your school’s instructions. This rehearsal has no calculator, digital research or AI service. Full mode locks access to other app study screens until submission; this is self-managed practice, not secure assessment software.

The official 2026 question/stimulus book contains both topic options; a candidate answers only the selected topic’s questions. This app deliberately shows the chosen topic only. Its cards are app-authored abridged evidence digests of cited records, not authentic verbatim extracts, facsimiles or unseen QCAA stimulus. Context and source type refer to the underlying record. Use teacher-supplied authentic texts, images, cartoons and graphs for further practice.

Verified 17 October 2026 against Modern History 2025 v1.4, the July 2026 example and memo 031/26. Nominated aspects apply in 2026–2027; later cohorts must check QCAA notices.

Class topics and v2 migration

Keep the two class study areas separate. Topic 1 and its aspect must differ from the selected external-assessment topic and aspect. Changing topics archives the previous ratings, drafts, notes and teacher set rather than deleting them.

Bring forward your v2 work

The original v2 storage is left untouched. Import ratings, drafts, class topics, planner and sets from this browser or a v2 JSON backup. Existing v3 data takes priority when the same variant already has a record. Browser file-origin storage may differ between filenames: export a backup from the old file when needed. Exam attempts keep their original separate key and are included in the v3 backup.
