UNIT 4 REFERENCE

Revolutions in modern physics

Special relativity

Revise inertial frames, Einstein's postulates, relativity of simultaneity, time dilation, length contraction, relativistic momentum and mass–energy equivalence, including interpretation of experimental evidence.

Quantum theory

Revise black-body radiation, photons, photoelectric effect, atomic models, line spectra, the Rydberg relationship and de Broglie matter waves.

The Standard Model

Revise quarks, leptons, hadrons, gauge bosons, fundamental interactions, particle/antiparticle ideas and conservation laws.

IA3 — Research Investigation

Unit 4 contains the summative Research Investigation. The companion in this app helps students move from a teacher-provided claim to a researchable question, credible secondary evidence, analysis, evidence-quality evaluation and a justified judgment about the broader claim.

External assessment

The Physics external assessment is worth 50% and assesses science-understanding subject matter from Units 3 and 4. This app only supplies the Unit 4 half of that preparation. Use the separate Unit 3 app for Gravity and electromagnetism.
