Exam selection

The 2026 syllabus fixes two written papers, each with 5 minutes’ perusal and 90 minutes’ working. It specifies question types, not a fixed 2026 raw-mark split. This simulator uses a labelled 100-mark, 50/50 practice blueprint.

Permitted materials: a QCAA-approved scientific or graphics calculator. Question formats may include multiple choice, single words, sentences/paragraphs, algorithmic calculations and unseen graphs, tables or diagrams. Extended-response questions are not used.
