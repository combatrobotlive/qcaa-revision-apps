Start practising

This app focuses on what students can actually improve before the exam: unpacking unseen questions, forming a discriminating thesis, selecting remembered evidence, analysing writer's choices, planning coherent arguments and writing analytically.

Your text

What the EA rewards

✓ Interpretation, not plot summary. Analyse representations, underlying ideas and writer's choices.
✓ A discriminating thesis. Take a clear, qualified position on the exact unseen question.
✓ Evidence used analytically. Select remembered textual evidence and explain how it strengthens an argument.
✓ Control of the essay. Logical sequencing, cohesion and purposeful academic language.

Exam conditions

15 minutes planning + 120 minutes working. Students respond to one unseen question on their studied EA text in an analytical essay. The studied text and notes are not taken into the examination.

Study note: use the evidence bank to practise recall now — not as material to take into the exam.

Text-specific planning drill

Choose a focus or generate a random combination. The cues are designed to trigger recall — not supply a pre-written interpretation.

Recall rule: use these cues to test what you know. Do not turn them into a memorised essay that ignores the unseen question.
