Unit reference

Concise prompts for revision—not a replacement for the syllabus or class materials.

Carbohydrate classification

Monosaccharides, disaccharides, oligosaccharides and polysaccharides differ in structure and behaviour. Carbohydrate supplies energy and can contribute structure, viscosity and water management.

Carbohydrate processes

  • Gelatinisation
  • Crystallisation and nucleation
  • Caramelisation
  • Dextrinisation
  • Gelification
  • Biological, chemical and physical leavening

Carbohydrate deterioration

Relevant changes include fermentation, retrogradation, syneresis and moisture loss. Processing and storage change access, shelf life and sensory quality.

Fat classification

Distinguish saturated, monounsaturated, polyunsaturated and trans fat. Link structure and physical state to nutritional and functional implications.

Fat food science

  • Emulsions and emulsifiers
  • Viscosity
  • Powderising
  • Spherification
  • Shortening and aeration
  • Oxidative rancidity and chocolate bloom

Experimental evidence

  1. Change a clearly defined independent variable.
  2. Control formulation and processing variables.
  3. Repeat measurements and report useful spread.
  4. Use sensory tests that answer the investigation question.
  5. Evaluate against success criteria.
