2026 changes built in: students are expected to calculate both r and R² using technology; critical path analysis is specified as activity on arc; and the Hungarian algorithm can be used to minimise or maximise up to 5×5 matrices.
EA structure: Paper 1 contains simple-familiar multiple-choice and short-response questions; Paper 2 contains complex-familiar and complex-unfamiliar short responses. Both allow a QCAA-approved scientific calculator and the QCAA formula book, with 5 minutes perusal + 90 minutes working time.
2025 format reference: Paper 1 used 15 MC + 10 short-response questions (57 raw marks). Paper 2 used 7 short-response questions (38 raw marks). The practice-paper buttons mirror that broad format.
