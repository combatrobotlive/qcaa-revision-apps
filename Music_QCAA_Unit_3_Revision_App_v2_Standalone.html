Unit 3 assessment support

IA1 Performance — 20%

Performance, live or recorded, up to 5 minutes. Practise technical control, stylistically informed interpretation and realisation of music ideas.

IA2 Composition — 20%

Composition of at least 1 minute plus a statement of intent: written up to 500 words, or spoken up to 3 minutes. The composition must demonstrate an innovation.

External assessment — 25%

Unseen question option(s) and succinct unseen stimulus; analytical essay; 20 minutes planning and 120 minutes working. Practise analysing relationships, evaluating significance and justifying a viewpoint with precise musical evidence.

Syllabus boundary: Unit 3 is summative: IA1 Performance (20%), IA2 Composition (20%). The external assessment is a 25% written extended response with unseen question/stimulus; this app focuses its EA practice on Unit 3 Innovations.

extended harmony

extended harmony, chromaticism, tone clusters and other expanded harmonic practices

modal

modal, pentatonic, diatonic, atonal, non-Western and other scale forms encountered in repertoire

innovative structure

innovative structure, including looping, phasing, additive processes and unconventional formal design

innovative texture

innovative texture, layering and density

innovative sound production

innovative sound production, acoustic/electronic timbre and technology extending musical possibilities
