ASSESSMENT PREPARATION

Unit 3 assessment support

IA1 · Examination — short response · 20%

IA1 relates to Unit 3 Topic 1. The examination has five short-response questions posed in English. Three responses are in English and two are in Italian. Questions cover PACT elements plus analysis/evaluation of unseen Italian stimulus texts.

The practice in this app uses original, synthetic stimuli for skill rehearsal; it is not an official or authentic QCAA instrument.

IA2 · Examination — extended response · 25%

IA2 relates to Unit 3 Topic 2 and/or Topic 3. It includes a written extended response in Italian with three different focuses, plus a spoken extended response in Italian involving a one-to-one student-centred conversation and unseen open-ended questions.

IA1 habits

  • Identify exactly the required PACT element.
  • Use distinct evidence from the Italian stimulus.
  • Move from evidence to a valid conclusion rather than paraphrasing only.
  • Match the response language requested.

IA2 habits

  • Address all three written focuses.
  • Use varied vocabulary and grammar with an appropriate register.
  • Sequence and synthesise ideas rather than listing them.
  • Practise spontaneous spoken follow-up rather than memorised answers.
