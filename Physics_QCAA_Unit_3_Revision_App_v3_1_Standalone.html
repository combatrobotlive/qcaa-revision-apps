Symbols, units and conversions

SI conversion routine
  1. Write the given value and prefix.
  2. Convert to the base SI unit before substitution.
  3. Keep powers of ten visible until the final step.
  4. Check the final unit and sensible magnitude.
Common Unit 3 traps
  • micro = 10-6, nano = 10-9, milli = 10-3.
  • Orbital/gravity r is measured from the centre of the source mass.
  • Magnetic-flux angle is measured to the area normal; force angles are between the two vectors.
  • N is turns; n is turn density in m-1.

Guide scope and source notes

The official formula/data book contains content from the whole Physics course. This Unit 3 app foregrounds the Gravity and motion and Electromagnetism entries, plus data processing and one useful Waves relationship. Other pages remain visible in the sheet map so students learn the real book layout without implying that every printed formula belongs to Unit 3.

Teaching-only vector and incline relationships are explicitly labelled as not separately printed in the QCAA formula book. They are included because the Unit 3 syllabus requires students to resolve and recombine vectors and analyse inclined-plane forces.
