REFERENCE

Spanish EA guide

Current Unit 4 scope

Topic 1 — The present: end of school, graduation, technology/sustainability/innovation, social and global events, volunteering.

Topic 2 — Future choices: employment and second-language opportunities, post-school options and independence, Spanish in study/travel/careers, global citizenship and maintaining relationships.

External assessment conditions

  • 5 minutes perusal and 120 minutes working time.
  • Three to six authentic Spanish stimuli related to Unit 4.
  • Combined Spanish stimulus length up to 1000 words.
  • At least one written and one audio/audiovisual text.
  • Responses may be in English (up to 100 words), Spanish short response (up to 100 words) or extended Spanish (up to 400 words).
  • No notes, dictionaries or reference materials.

2025 paper — useful practice pattern

  • Section 1: 14 marks — two short responses in Spanish based on audio.
  • Section 2: 17 marks — four short responses in English based on two written Spanish texts.
  • Section 3: 21 marks — one extended response in Spanish.

2025 subject-report advice built into this app

  • Use each piece of evidence from a stimulus only once across the examination.
  • Practise identifying gendered characters and preserve gender and tense accurately.
  • Make it routine to judge whether an example really justifies the conclusion.
  • When responding in English, translate the meaning of any Spanish quotation into English.
  • When responding in Spanish, keep the entire response in Spanish and maintain consistent register.
  • For context, identify the actual scenario/situation, not simply the text type.
