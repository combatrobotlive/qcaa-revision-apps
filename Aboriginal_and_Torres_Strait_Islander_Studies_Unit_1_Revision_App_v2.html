Best way to use this app

Choose a topic or skill, plan your answer before revealing the guidance, then rate how independently you could do it. Keep every claim specific to the source, historical context and question.

Use your class sources and local perspectives to deepen this practice. Do not treat generic information as a replacement for the authority of the relevant peoples and communities.

Unit boundary: this standalone V2 app follows the QCAA 2025 v1.3 Unit 1 subject matter. It provides original revision prompts and is not a QCAA publication or cultural authority.

Response toolkit

Source skills and planning

Interpret sources

  • Identify a precise feature of the source.
  • Explain the meaning you draw from it.
  • Link that meaning to the exact question.
  • Keep evidence and inference distinct.

Analyse perspective

  • Identify viewpoint, position, purpose or assumptions.
  • Explain how it shapes representation or response.
  • Use the historical, social and cultural context.
  • Do not label a source “biased” without explaining its effect.

Evaluate significance

  • Make a clear and qualified judgment.
  • Use criteria such as scope, depth, duration and consequence.
  • Weigh evidence and limits relevant to this inquiry.
  • Return to the wording of the question.

Use respectful language

  • Use the terms preferred by the relevant people and place.
  • Be specific about peoples, communities and locations.
  • Avoid deficit framing and generalisations.
  • Respect cultural authority, permissions and restricted knowledge.
