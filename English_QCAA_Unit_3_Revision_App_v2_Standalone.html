Textual connections

Scope: aligned to English General 2025 v1.3 (January 2026). This app is an independent revision resource, not a QCAA publication. Use the current syllabus, school task documents, QCAA prescribed text lists and subject notices as the final authority where applicable.

Topic 1 — Conversations about issues in texts

Analyse different perspectives on the same issue across texts, including media texts, and develop a spoken persuasive argument that adds to public dialogue.

Topic 2 — Conversations about concepts in texts

Study two different types of texts connected by a concept, identity, time or place, or through adaptation/transformation, and contribute to public conversations about their representations.

Textual connections

Examine how texts resonate, relate to or clash with one another through context, form, language, assumptions, values and audience positioning.

Key vocabulary

contentionThe central persuasive position the speaker wants an audience to accept.
public audienceAn identified audience beyond an assumed classroom marker, with its own context and expectations.
rhetoricStrategic use of language and structure to influence an audience.
textual connectionA meaningful relationship between texts that can create new understanding.
comparative lensA focused interpretive perspective that controls comparison between texts.
synthesisIntegrated reasoning in which comparison produces a new insight rather than separate mini-analyses.

High-value revision routine

  1. Recall the concept or text evidence before opening notes.
  2. Answer the exact question in your own words.
  3. Reveal the checking guide and identify the missing reasoning.
  4. Revise the same response or retry a new A/B/C variant without copying a model.
