Three practice modes: written A questions explain concepts, B questions apply them, and C questions challenge a misconception. Calculations and data questions use reproducible seeded numbers; MC versions reorder the choices. The 576 count is practice versions, not 576 unrelated skills.
Current EA scope: the external assessment samples science understanding from all Units 3 and 4. That means animal production, plant production and agricultural enterprises from Unit 3, plus enterprise management and sustainability from Unit 4.
2026 format: two written papers, each with 5 minutes perusal + 90 minutes working time, with an approved scientific or graphics calculator. this app emphasises concise explanation, calculation, data analysis and justified conclusions.
Revision priorities: learn all parts of multi-part syllabus statements (especially pest/disease control), use precise agricultural terminology, practise complex relationships across graphs/tables, and clearly distinguish animal welfare from animal ethics.

Course and assessment guide

Course and assessment map

Unit 1: Agricultural systems. Unit 2: Resources. Unit 3: Agricultural production. Unit 4: Agricultural management.

IA1 data test: 10%; IA2 student experiment: 20%; IA3 research investigation: 20%; external examination: 50%. The data-test practice uses 3 datasets and 30 raw practice marks, with a 9/9/12 split across application, analysis and interpretation. These raw marks are not an IA score.

Source checked 10 September 2026: QCAA Agricultural Science 2025 v1.3 syllabus. Follow the school task sheet for your own IA requirements.

What can be externally assessed?

Unit 3 Topic 1: Animal production B — nutrition, growth/development, health, markets, ethics and welfare.

Unit 3 Topic 2: Plant production B — agronomy, plant health, pest/disease/weed management, hormones, post-harvest, propagation and breeding.

Unit 3 Topic 3: Agricultural enterprises B — products, marketing, quality assurance, supply/demand, trade and post-harvest quality.

Unit 4 Topic 1: Enterprise management — records, budgets, gross margins, management decisions, risk and PPRR.

Unit 4 Topic 2: Sustainability — environmental, financial and social factors.

Current examination conditions

  • External assessment weighting: 50%.
  • Two written papers.
  • Each paper: 5 min perusal + 90 min working.
  • QCAA-approved graphics or scientific calculator permitted.
  • Responses may include MC, single words, sentences/paragraphs, calculations and unseen graphs/tables/diagrams.

Useful response habits

  • When a syllabus statement lists several methods, learn and practise all of them — e.g. chemical, physical, biological, management and IPM controls.
  • Use precise terms such as IPM, nematicide, gross margin, biosecurity and quality assurance.
  • When analysing data, identify a specific relationship and support it with values/units rather than describing the graph vaguely.
  • For welfare vs ethics: welfare is about measurable animal wellbeing; ethics is about judgments concerning what ought to be done.
  • Use the agricultural context named in the question; generic examples often lose precision.
