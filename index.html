How to use the revision apps

Choose the question format that suits your revision.

1. Choose your subject and unit

Use the search, category filters or Year 11/12 shortcuts. Click a star to pin your subject in My revision for next time.

2. Practise at your level

Simple Practice covers straightforward questions. Complex Practice includes more involved calculations, reasoning and written responses.

3. Try exam-style questions

Practice Exam is inside each unit app. Where available, Practice EA · Units 3 + 4 is a separate combined external-assessment simulator.

Teacher Tools, saving work and backup details

Individual v3.1 apps include Teacher Tools, which may offer custom exam building, printable material and set codes. Check each app for its exact options. Browser-stored answers and settings generally remain on that device. The homepage's Download/Restore feature saves localStorage for the current website origin only; it does not include IndexedDB or automatically combine saves from other website addresses. Use the app's own export or backup option when available.

What's new · 18 November 2026

Every Queensland unit is now v3.1

All 41 subjects have Unit 1–4 links. This hub adds favourites, a three-app recent history, selected topic shortcuts, Year 11/12 views and a safer localStorage backup/restore workflow. No account is required. Previous app versions may use different save keys, so keep an old backup before switching.
