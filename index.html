ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built for students. Shared with teachers.

Free educational use

The apps are shared free for educational use. Please keep the creator attribution when redistributing the files.

Teacher-led development

Created by Daniel Kerrison, Engineering Teacher at Southport State High School, through classroom use, testing, correction and iteration.

AI-assisted development

Development was assisted by ChattyJeeps (ChatGPT by OpenAI). The apps were iteratively specified, reviewed and tested rather than produced from a single prompt.
