Model checks and boundaries

The generator checks global equilibrium, truss joint equilibrium, support contact, solvable geometry, load positions, and consistency between shear and bending moment. Invalid combinations are explained, not silently changed. Only point loads are used. All beam loads act on the centreline. Truss loads act at joints. No distributed-load integration or indeterminate structures are introduced.

For beams, symmetrical loading is mirrored about the support midpoint (the overall midpoint for a cantilever exercise). A symmetrical truss needs mirrored joint positions. A single angled load or an odd number of horizontal loads cannot form a mirrored loading pattern: use asymmetrical loading instead.

Equilibrium and structures

Force equilibriumΣFx = 0
ΣFy = 0Resolve angled forces before applying equilibrium.
Moment equilibriumΣM = 0
M = F d⊥Choose a moment centre that removes unknown reactions where possible.
Method of jointsΣFx = 0
ΣFy = 0Start at a joint with no more than two unknown member forces. Assume tension first; a negative result indicates compression.
Method of sectionsΣFx = 0
ΣFy = 0
ΣM = 0Cut through no more than three unknown members and take moments to eliminate two where possible.
Point-load SFDShear changes by the size of each vertical point force.Between point forces, shear is constant.
Point-load BMDdM/dx follows shearFor this course's point-load cases, bending moment varies linearly between loads and is zero at a simply supported beam end without an applied end couple. It need not be zero at an interior support of an overhanging beam.

Materials and member checks

Normal stressσ = F / A1 MPa = 1 N/mm². State tension or compression where relevant.
Strainε = ΔL / LUse consistent length units; strain has no unit.
Young's modulusE = σ / ε
ΔL = FL / AEUse the linear elastic region and consistent SI units.
Factor of safetyFoS = failure stress / allowable stressUse yield stress or another specified failure criterion.
Ultimate tensile strengthUTS = maximum load / original areaUse the original cross-sectional area.
Common conversions1 kN = 1000 N
1 MPa = 1 N/mm²
1 GPa = 1000 MPaMoment fields accept equivalent N m or kN m units.

A repeatable statics method

  1. Sketch or inspect the structure and identify the body or joint to analyse.
  2. Draw the free-body diagram and replace supports with correct reaction components.
  3. Resolve angled forces and write known dimensions beside the diagram.
  4. Select equilibrium equations in an order that minimises unknowns.
  5. Check sign, units, direction and whether the result is physically reasonable.
  6. For member design, continue from force to stress, allowable stress or factor of safety.

Checking answers

Use one number per field. Examples: 12.5 kN, 12500 N, 36 kN m, 150 MPa. A bare number uses the unit shown beside the field. Most numerical checks allow 1.5% rounding tolerance. Automatic marks check final answers; students should still show working for assessment preparation.

The generated 50-mark mock is a revision blueprint with 30 simple-familiar marks, 10 complex-familiar marks and one 10-mark coherent CU scenario. Boundaries

The app intentionally avoids distributed-load integration, statically indeterminate structures, advanced frames and machines, and university-level vector formalism. It focuses on the beam, truss, SFD/BMD and materials calculations explicitly required for Unit 3.

Exam and IA1 boundary

IA2 uses objectives 1, 2, 3 and 5 in structural contexts. Project criteria, prototype manufacture and evaluation remain useful IA1 support but are not a substitute for the IA2 response demands. The EA directly assesses Unit 4, not an equal Unit 3/4 split.
