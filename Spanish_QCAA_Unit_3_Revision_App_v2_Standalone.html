Assessment support

IA1 and IA2 support

Unit 3 has two prescribed internal assessments. This app practises the communication demands without reproducing official QCAA instruments or stimuli.

Assessment support

IA1 · Short response · 20%

Topic 1. Practise five English short responses to three unseen authentic Spanish stimuli. The published conditions use up to 1000 words across stimuli, include written and audio/audiovisual material, and allow 5 minutes perusal plus 90 minutes working time.

Assessment support

IA2 · Extended response · 25%

Topic 2 and/or Topic 3. Practise an open-ended response using a written Spanish text followed by a conversation in Spanish. Focus on synthesis, justified viewpoints, register and interaction.

Assessment support

Evidence discipline

For both tasks, tie every interpretation to specific stimulus evidence; distinguish what the text states from what you infer.

Use: this section is revision support only. Follow your school’s task sheet and the current QCAA syllabus/assessment specifications for formal assessment.
Reference

Unit 3 language and response toolkit

Quick reminders for interpreting and creating Spanish in this unit.

IA1 reading/listening workflow

1) Identify context and audience. 2) Mark main ideas. 3) Select precise evidence. 4) Explain what the evidence shows. 5) Check that the response answers the English question.

IA2 response planning

Build a clear stance, synthesise stimulus ideas, add your own relevant perspective, link paragraphs, then prepare to clarify and extend ideas orally.

Analytical language

el texto sugiere…, se observa que…, el contraste entre… indica…, la perspectiva del autor…, esto pone de relieve…

Nuanced cultural claims

Use evidence-led qualifiers such as según el estímulo, en esta comunidad, para algunas personas and puede indicar rather than absolute claims.
