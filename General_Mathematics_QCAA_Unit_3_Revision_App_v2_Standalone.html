Example: verify and evaluate a model

A fitted line predicts 72 units. Substituting the same input into an independently constructed spreadsheet checks the arithmetic. Comparing with a held-out observed value of 68 checks predictive performance: the error is 4 units, or about 5.9% of the observed value. These are different checks.

If removing an influential observation changes the recommendation, discuss that sensitivity. A high correlation alone does not verify predictions or establish causation.
