Important terminology: students often call the project evidence a “folio”, but QCAA's 2025 response requirements use Design brief, Design proposal and Design process. The Design process is not meant to contain everything produced; it should be a purposeful representation of the authentic process.

IA2 — Human-centred design

Approximately 20 hours class time. Design brief up to 600 words on one A3 page; spoken + visual Design proposal up to 3 minutes; Design process up to 10 A3 pages with up to 500 words of annotations on developed ideas.

IA3 — Sustainable redesign

Approximately 15 hours class time. Design brief up to 500 words on one A3 page; one-A3-page visual Design proposal; Design process up to eight A3 pages with up to 400 words of annotations on developed ideas.
