2026 emphasis: the revised syllabus wording explicitly asks students to explain how geographic, temporal and spatial isolation influence gene flow and may lead to allopatric, sympatric and parapatric speciation. The bank therefore gives speciation and gene-flow reasoning substantial practice.
EA structure: two papers, each with 5 minutes perusal and 90 minutes working time. A QCAA-approved scientific or graphics calculator is permitted.
2025 format reference: Paper 1 contained 20 multiple-choice marks plus 29 short-response marks; Paper 2 contained 52 short-response marks. Practice-paper generators mirror that broad structure as a reference, not a prediction of exact 2026 raw marks.
