What is the examiner asking?

Subject-specific tools

Read before calculating

  1. Identify the requested interval, branch, test row or phase.
  2. Copy only the required readings with their units.
  3. Keep the full precision until the final answer.
  4. Compare the result with the actual requirement.

Reading a figure is part of the skill, not optional decoration.

Motion, MA and VR

Average velocity uses displacement and the whole elapsed time, including stops. Acceleration uses a change in velocity. MA compares forces; VR compares travel distances during the same motion. Use consistent units.

Two-resistor circuits

Trace the wires before selecting series or parallel. Series shares current; parallel shares voltage. Use V=IR, P=VI and E=Pt for the relevant component or source. Seconds pair with watts to give joules.

Logic and truth tables

Define 0/1 input meanings, label every input/output, and list all combinations. Evaluate brackets and NOT conditions before the final gate. AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR and XOR are included; automatic checking is by row.

Cu-Ni evidence

Read liquidus/solidus and horizontal tie-line endpoints. Keep phase composition distinct from mass fraction. Use the opposite segment in the lever rule; fractions must sum to one. App curves are explicitly schematic teaching models.

Evidence-based proposals

State a recommendation, quote relevant data, compare it with constraints, then identify a limitation or validation need. Consider ethical, social, economic and environmental effects as the task requires.

Practical/project support

Determine measurable success criteria, plan a prototype, control test conditions, repeat tests, evaluate results and justify refinements. The quiz can rehearse analysis and planning; it cannot replace actual prototype generation or performance evidence required by your school task.

Calculator and response checks

1 kJ=1000 J; 1 kWh=3,600,000 J; 1 mm=0.001 m. For parallel resistance, invert the sum of reciprocals. Enter one final number per field. Preserve your working: the app checks final responses, not official method marks. The real school task defines calculator/reference permissions.

Unit boundary

Engineering 2025 v1.4 Unit 2 covers emerging needs, processes/machinery/automation and materials. Do not import Unit 4 screw ratios, efficiencies, Pb-Sn or steel heat-treatment questions into this Unit 2 rehearsal. Annealing and hot/cold processing remain Unit 2 content.
