20-MINUTE REVISION

Recall → interpret → create → repair

  1. Recall useful vocabulary and one sentence pattern without notes.
  2. Attempt a comprehension or analysis question.
  3. Create a short Chinese response using evidence or a justified perspective.
  4. Check the guide, record one repair, then revisit later.
LANGUAGE DISCIPLINE

Meaning first, then precision

Answer the question actually asked, support interpretations with details from the text, and use Chinese that fits the context and audience. For created responses, accuracy matters, but so do ideas, structure and purposeful language choices.

PRACTICE HABITS

Use language as meaning-making

  • Choose patterns because they express the relationship you need: cause, comparison, concession, condition, advice or evaluation.
  • Prefer a clear, accurate sentence over a complicated sentence you cannot control.
  • Check character choice, word order and whether the register suits the audience.
  • When interpreting, distinguish what the text states from what you infer.

REFERENCE

Unit 2 response guide

Six syllabus objectives

    Interpretation checklist

    • Locate explicit details before inferring.
    • For tone/purpose/audience, cite a language or content feature.
    • When analysing two ideas, explain the relationship rather than summarising separately.
    • Qualify broad cultural claims; a text may present one perspective, not an entire community.
    • For data, state what the figures show before explaining what they might mean.

    Created response checklist

    • Address every part of the prompt.
    • Use examples/details that develop the idea.
    • Link ideas with purposeful Chinese sentence patterns.
    • Justify opinions instead of only stating preferences.
    • Proofread high-frequency characters, measure words, aspect and word order.
