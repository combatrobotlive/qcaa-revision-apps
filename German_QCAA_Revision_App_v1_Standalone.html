REFERENCE

German EA guide

Current Unit 4 scope

Topic 1 — The present: end of school, graduation, technology/sustainability/innovation, social and global events, volunteering.

Topic 2 — Future choices: employment and second-language opportunities, post-school options and independence, German in study/travel/careers, global citizenship and maintaining relationships.

External assessment conditions

  • 5 minutes perusal and 120 minutes working time.
  • Three to six authentic German stimuli related to Unit 4.
  • Combined German stimulus length up to 1000 words.
  • At least one written and one audio/audiovisual text.
  • Responses may be in English (up to 100 words), German short response (up to 100 words) or extended German (up to 400 words).
  • No notes, dictionaries or reference materials.

2025 paper — useful practice pattern

  • Section 1: 14 marks — two short responses in German based on audio.
  • Section 2: 15 marks — four short responses in English based on two written German texts.
  • Section 3: 21 marks — one extended response in German.

2025 subject-report advice

  • Read the exact question carefully and include all required information.
  • Evidence marks are not secure when the stimulus has been misinterpreted.
  • For context, explain the situation in which the text exists rather than naming only the text type.
  • Structure extended writing into logical paragraphs; introduction and conclusion are useful where appropriate.
  • Higher language-element marks require a wide range of vocabulary, grammar and tenses, not just accurate simple German.
