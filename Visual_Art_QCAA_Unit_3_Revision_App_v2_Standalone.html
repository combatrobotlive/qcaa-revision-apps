VISUAL ART · UNIT 3

Art as knowledge

Build a sustained self-directed body of work from a direct stimulus or first-hand experience. Practise generating an individual inquiry question, constructing knowledge through research and experimentation, evaluating influence and synthesising evidence into resolved meaning.
