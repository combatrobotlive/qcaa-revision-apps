REFERENCE

Italian EA guide

Current Unit 4 scope

Topic 1 — The present: end of school, graduation, technology/sustainability/innovation, social and global events, volunteering.

Topic 2 — Future choices: employment and second-language opportunities, post-school options and independence, Italian in study/travel/careers, global citizenship and maintaining relationships.

External assessment conditions

  • 5 minutes perusal and 120 minutes working time.
  • Three to six authentic Italian stimuli related to Unit 4.
  • Combined Italian stimulus length up to 1000 words.
  • At least one written and one audio/audiovisual text.
  • Responses may be in English (up to 100 words), Italian short response (up to 100 words) or extended Italian (up to 400 words).
  • No notes, dictionaries or reference materials.

2025 paper — useful practice pattern

  • Section 1: 15 marks — two short responses in Italian based on audio.
  • Section 2: 14 marks — four short responses in English based on two written Italian texts.
  • Section 3: 21 marks — one extended response in Italian; the paper asked for 200–300 words.

2025 subject-report advice built into this app

  • For context, explain the environment/situation: who, what, where, when and why the communication exists — not just the text type.
  • For a how/why question, state the manner or reason explicitly before giving evidence.
  • Notice gender clues in Italian and use the correct pronouns when referring to the writer/speaker.
  • Use different evidence in different responses on the same stimulus and paraphrase in full sentences.
  • Keep short responses direct: conclusion first, then supporting examples.
  • Use clear paragraphs in the extended response and develop the required focus areas separately.
  • Showcase complex language from the mandatory language elements and maintain anonymity in exam responses.
