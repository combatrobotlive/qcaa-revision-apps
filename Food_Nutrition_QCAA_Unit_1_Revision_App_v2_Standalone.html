Unit reference

Concise prompts for revision—not a replacement for the syllabus or class materials.

Food system

  • Production
  • Processing
  • Distribution
  • Consumption
  • Research and development

Sustainability, waste management and food protection affect every sector.

Nutrient groups

Macronutrients: protein, carbohydrate, fat, water and fibre.

Fat-soluble vitamins: A, D, E and K.

Water-soluble vitamins: B1, B2, B3 and C.

Minerals: calcium, sodium and iron.

Vitamins and minerals

  • Distinguish amount present from bioavailability.
  • Compare raw, fresh, frozen, dehydrated and canned sources.
  • Explain how processing affects nutrients, sensory quality and shelf life.
  • Use nutrient reference values for the correct age and life stage.

Protein food science

  • Proteins consist of amino acids.
  • Animal and plant sources can both contribute protein.
  • Processes include aeration, denaturation, Maillard browning and coagulation.
  • Heat, pH, enzymes, salts and manipulation can alter protein structure and food quality.

Sensory evidence

Describe appearance, taste, texture, aroma and flavour. Match the sensory method to the question, control serving conditions and use the intended consumer group where preference matters.

Strong explanation

  1. Name the nutrient or functional property.
  2. Explain the mechanism.
  3. Connect it to nutrition, structure, sensory quality or shelf life.
  4. State evidence that would test the claim.
