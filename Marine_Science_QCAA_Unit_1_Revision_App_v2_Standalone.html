UNIT 1 DATA PRACTICE

Short marine-data scenarios

All figures are simulated practice data. Use the prompts, then reveal an indicative answer.

Coastal sediment budget

A simulated beach receives 310 m³ of sediment and loses 390 m³ in one month.

Try: Calculate net change; then interpret the sign.

Indicative answer

310 − 390 = −80 m³. This is a net sediment loss.

Water quality and BOD

A controlled sample begins at 8.6 mg/L dissolved oxygen and finishes at 3.7 mg/L.

Try: Calculate oxygen consumed; explain what a higher value can indicate.

Indicative answer

8.6 − 3.7 = 4.9 mg/L. Greater oxygen consumption can indicate greater organic pollution under comparable conditions.

Wave celerity

A wave has a wavelength of 60 m and a period of 7 s.

Try: Calculate wave celerity.

Indicative answer

60 / 7 = 8.57 m/s.
