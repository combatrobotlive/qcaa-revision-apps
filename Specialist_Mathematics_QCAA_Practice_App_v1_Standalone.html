2026 syllabus changes: graphing regions in the complex plane has moved to Unit 2 and is assumed knowledge; multi-angle trigonometric identities have moved to Unit 3 and may be directly assessed; the formula book has been updated.
EA structure: Paper 1 is technology-free and Paper 2 is technology-active. Each has 5 minutes perusal + 90 minutes working time. In 2025, each paper contained 10 marks of multiple choice and 50 marks of short response (60 raw marks per paper).
Expression checking: for selected algebra/calculus questions, enter expressions using * for multiplication and ^ for powers, e.g. 2*x*exp(x). Equivalent forms are checked numerically at several values of x.
