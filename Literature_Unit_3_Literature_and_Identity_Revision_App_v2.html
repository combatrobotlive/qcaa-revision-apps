How to use this app well

Practise from memory

For each task, recall precise scenes, shots, words, structural choices or sound details from your own class texts.

Write interpretations, not summaries

A literary response needs a claim about how meaning is constructed—not merely what happens.

Keep work ethical

Use the planning and self-check tools to strengthen your own thinking and drafting.
