Historical source skills

Analyse evidence

  • Select a specific feature of the source.
  • Explain what the evidence reveals in historical context.
  • Examine relationships: cause, consequence, continuity/change, significance or perspective.
  • Connect the evidence directly to the question.

Evaluate usefulness & reliability

  • Judge usefulness for the specific inquiry, not in general.
  • Use origin, purpose, audience, context and perspective.
  • Examine what the source can and cannot show.
  • Corroborate or challenge with other evidence.

Synthesise

  • Make a clear historical claim.
  • Combine evidence from multiple sources rather than summarising one source at a time.
  • Acknowledge competing explanations or perspectives.
  • Make and justify an overall judgment.

Comprehend in context

  • Use accurate historical terms.
  • Place terms and evidence in the correct period and circumstances.
  • Explain the issue, not just define a word.
  • Avoid unsupported generalisations.
