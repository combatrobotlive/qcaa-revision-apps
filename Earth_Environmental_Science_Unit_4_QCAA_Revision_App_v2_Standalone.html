UNIT 4 REFERENCE

Unit 4 course guide

Assessment pathway

  • IA3 Research investigation — 20%: evaluate a teacher-provided claim relevant to Unit 4 using secondary evidence.
  • External assessment: Unit 4 is examined with Unit 3 in two written papers.

High-value habits

  • Link the Earth process to the hazard, then distinguish physical event magnitude from consequences.
  • For climate evidence, identify the trend, supporting values and limitations before forming a conclusion.
  • Do not treat an average recurrence interval, climate driver or projection as a guarantee.
