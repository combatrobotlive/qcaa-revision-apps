REFERENCE

Reliable response pattern

  1. Identify the economic issue and relevant evidence.
  2. Define the key concept precisely.
  3. Explain the relationship using a model, diagram or causal chain.
  4. Apply the idea to the specific context.
  5. For evaluation, use an economic criterion and make a qualified decision.
