PHYSICAL EDUCATION v1

QCAA external-assessment practice

This app is focused on Unit 4 — Energy, fitness and training in physical activity, the current externally assessed PE unit.

360question variants
120question families
12generated performance stimuli
2025 EA reference: one 55-mark paper — 10 multiple choice (10 marks), three short-answer questions (28 marks), and one extended response (17 marks). The current conditions are 5 minutes perusal + 120 minutes working.
2026 changes built in: physiological responses to training are explicitly assessable; perusal has reduced from 15 to 5 minutes; references to “implications” were removed from Unit 4 assessment specifications.

Core exam habit: identify the movement demand → select significant stimulus data → connect energy/fitness/training concepts → evaluate strengths and limitations → devise and justify a specific training decision.

Unofficial practice tool. Not produced or endorsed by QCAA.
