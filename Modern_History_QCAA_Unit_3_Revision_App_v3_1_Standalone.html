Set up a Unit 3 skills rehearsal

The real EA has one written short-response paper: 15 minutes planning and 120 minutes working, on the selected Unit 4 topic/aspect. This app’s long option uses that clock for adapted Unit 3 skills practice. The short timing, source set and 18 marks are teaching allocations. There are no official Part A/B/C sections or Unit 3 EA mark weights here.

The official examination is handwritten. Print the response book to practise handwriting. Standard Modern History conditions permit general approved stationery but no additional subject equipment, calculator, personal notes or digital research. Follow approved adjustments and your school’s instructions. Each card identifies its evidence type. New studies include short verified extracts, historical photographs and source tables; earlier digest and fictional method packs remain available. Use authentic teacher-supplied extracts for final unseen-source practice.

Content and migration audit

Preserved from the first v3.1 conversion: six cited-evidence families / 18 written variants. November source audit: six additional families / 18 variants, for 48 families / 144 variants; short primary extracts, one historical photograph, source tables, mixed-source inquiries and three new national rehearsal contexts; 12 source digests in Australia/Germany studies; one original AEC data chart; objective and skill mapping; reproducible Standard/Hard/Brutal source inquiries; timed Unit 3 source-skills rehearsals and optional whole-number self/teacher practice marks.

The original Unit 3 self-review arrangement is retained. No AI feedback/export feature is enabled. The standalone app filename is retained for replacement at the existing hub URL; its internal version is 3.1. Normal app download exports no progress or responses.

Coverage limitation: the cited national studies cover Australia, Germany and the United States. Other selected Unit 3 topics retain all original methods questions and adapted fictional source packs. Digests are not authentic verbatim extracts; use teacher-supplied originals for final unseen-source practice.

Class topics and v2 migration

Your school selects two national-experience topics. Choose two prescribed national-experience topics and the aspects actually studied by your class. Changing topics archives the previous ratings, drafts, notes and teacher set rather than deleting them.
