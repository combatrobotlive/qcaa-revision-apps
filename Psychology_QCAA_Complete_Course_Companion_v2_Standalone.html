Psychology-specific build: unlike Physics, this bank leans harder into terminology, classic studies, interpretation of behavioural scenarios, research validity, and evidence-based explanations. The Stats Lab covers mean/median/range/SD/SE/95% CI, Pearson r and paired/unpaired t-test calculations.
EA focus: the external assessment is Units 3 and 4 only. Unit 3 covers brain function, sensation and perception, memory and learning; Unit 4 covers social psychology, interpersonal processes, attitudes and cross-cultural psychology.

Research methods matter everywhere. The current syllabus explicitly includes participant selection/allocation, confounds, blinding, counterbalancing, standardised procedures, informed consent, confidentiality, withdrawal rights, deception/debriefing, descriptive statistics, Pearson r, t-tests, p-values, Type I/II error, reliability and internal/external validity.
Wellbeing: Psychology includes sensitive subject matter. Keep classroom discussion scientific and objective; students should not be required to disclose personal health status or behaviours.
