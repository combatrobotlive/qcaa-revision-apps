2026 emphasis: Unit 3 brain-function wording has been revised, and sensation/perception explicitly includes auditory perception alongside visual perception. This bank therefore gives substantial practice to hearing pathways, loudness/pitch/timbre and cultural influences on both visual and auditory perception.
EA structure: two papers covering Units 3 and 4, each with 5 minutes perusal and 90 minutes working time. An approved calculator is permitted.
2025 format reference: Paper 1 contained 20 multiple-choice marks plus 29 short-response marks; Paper 2 contained 46 short-response marks. Practice-paper generators mirror that broad structure as a reference, not a prediction of exact 2026 raw marks.
