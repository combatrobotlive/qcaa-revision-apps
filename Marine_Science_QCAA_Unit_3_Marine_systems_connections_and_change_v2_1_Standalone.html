Student Experiment Companion

1. Experiment → modification → research question

Treat the draft as a prompt. A strong research question must be specific, relevant and supported by a methodology capable of collecting sufficient data.

3. Repeated-measure uncertainty helper

Enter repeat measurements for one IV level. This calculates mean, half-range and half-range as a percentage of the mean — a common school-lab summary. Use the uncertainty method required by your teacher/instrument.

4. Linear trend helper

Enter comma-separated x and y values. The helper calculates a least-squares straight-line slope, intercept and R². A high R² does not by itself establish validity or causation.

5. Evaluation planner

A strong IA2 evaluation does not list generic improvements. Link the limitation → effect on evidence → specific improvement, then explain why the improvement helps.

Units 1–2 use school-developed assessment. Units 3–4 contribute IA1 Data test (10%), IA2 Student experiment (20%), IA3 Research investigation (20%) and external assessment (50%). EA science understanding comes from Units 3–4.

IA1: Unit 3; 5 minutes perusal and 60 minutes working; 2–4 unseen datasets. EA: two papers, each with 5 minutes perusal and 90 minutes working. A QCAA-approved graphics or scientific calculator is permitted. Practice sets here do not prescribe the official examination's question or raw-mark counts.

IA2 and IA3: individual responses, approximately 10 hours of class time; up to 2000 words written or 11 minutes multimodal. Use your teacher's task sheet and current marking guide.
