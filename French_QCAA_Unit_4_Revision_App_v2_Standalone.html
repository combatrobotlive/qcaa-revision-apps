HOW TO REVISE

Use the language, then analyse it

  1. Listen or read once for gist.
  2. Read again for evidence, PACT and viewpoint.
  3. Respond in the requested language and form.
  4. Reveal the checklist only after a genuine attempt.
  5. Flag weak families for another session.
