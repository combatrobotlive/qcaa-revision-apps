Prepare for the 2026 Drama external assessment

The 2026 format changes the task substantially: students respond as audience to unseen excerpts of professional recorded live performance, supplied digitally in the examination, and write an analytical essay that analyses, synthesises, evaluates and justifies a viewpoint.

Important: this app's performance packs are synthetic text-and-stage-diagram simulations. They train observation and reasoning, but they do not replace practice watching unfamiliar recorded live theatre. The real EA stimulus is audiovisual (MP4).

What the current EA assesses

Analyse

Deconstruct how dramatic languages work together to create dramatic action and meaning. Use precise description of what an audience sees and hears.

Evaluate

Make convincing judgments about how effectively dramatic languages communicate meaning, supported by detailed, credible performance evidence.

Synthesise

Build a consistent viewpoint across moments rather than writing disconnected technique paragraphs.

Apply literacy

Use accurate drama terminology and a sustained analytical essay structure.

2026 format shift

Schools no longer select and study a production from a prescribed recorded-live list. Students instead view short unseen excerpts under exam conditions.

Practice implication: train transfer. Students need to identify relationships between elements, stagecraft and conventions in theatre they have never seen before.

2025 marker advice carried forward

Describe moments precisely

Name what the actor, ensemble, space, light, sound or object actually does before interpreting it.

Show interrelationships

Avoid isolated technique spotting. Explain how dramatic languages combine to produce action and meaning.

Separate analysis from evaluation

Analysis explains how meaning is made; evaluation judges how effectively that meaning is communicated and why.

Elements of drama

Stagecraft

Analysis vs evaluation

Analysis
“The performer closes the distance between A and B while dropping vocal volume; the combination of space, relationship and tension turns the confrontation from public challenge into private threat.”
Evaluation
“This manipulation is highly effective because the sudden intimacy makes the threat more unsettling for the audience than the earlier shouting, clarifying the character's control.”
