Unit assessment support

Schools develop Unit 1 assessment within QCAA requirements. Use this planner to rehearse the Health inquiry model without assuming a single mandated task format.

Define and understand

Describe the narrow resilience issue, target context and relevant data. Analyse resources, stressors, barriers and enablers; critique determinants using the PERMA+ lens.

Plan and act

Complete a needs assessment, justify one PERMA+ focus, and design an ethical, feasible personal action linked to Ottawa Charter personal skills.

Evaluate and reflect

Use suitable pre/post evidence and RE-AIM to judge impact. Justify recommendations that advocate, mediate or enable further health promotion.

Quality checklist

Unit reference

Core language for accurate revision and assessment responses.

Resilience
Capacity to adapt and draw on resources when responding to stressors.
General resistance resource
A resource that supports movement toward the ease end of the health continuum.
Eustress
Stress interpreted as positive or useful for growth or performance.
Locus of control
A belief about whether outcomes are mainly shaped by one’s own actions or outside forces.
Developmental assets
External assets such as support and empowerment, and internal assets such as positive values and identity.
Neuroplasticity
The brain’s capacity to adapt and reorganise in response to experience.
Needs assessment
A synthesis of evidence used to identify a priority need, barrier or enabler.
Personal agency
A person’s capacity to make choices and act intentionally.

Cognitive verb moves

VerbWhat the response must do
AnalyseBreak information into parts, explain relationships and draw a conclusion.

CritiqueExamine information in detail through the relevant model to distinguish determinants.
SynthesiseCombine evidence into a new, coherent action strategy or decision.
EvaluateJudge methodology or resources against criteria such as RE-AIM.
ReflectAppraise impact or uptake and use the judgment to shape future action.
