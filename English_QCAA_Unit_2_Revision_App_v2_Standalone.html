Unit 2 focus

1. Culture and representation: examine whose perspectives are privileged, challenged or marginalised.
2. Contextual influence: explain how time, place and cultural conditions shape meaning.
3. Adaptation and transformation: analyse what changes when a text is reshaped for a new context or audience.
4. Comparative interpretation: move beyond separate summaries to meaningful synthesis.

Revision habits built into this app

- Answer the exact question rather than a memorised version of a familiar theme.
- Connect representation, values and textual choices through reasoning, not isolated technique labels.
- Select evidence because it advances an interpretation.
- Use guidance to revise your own work rather than copying a model response.
