Scope: aligned to English General 2025 v1.3 (January 2026). This app is an independent revision resource, not a QCAA publication. Use the current syllabus, school task documents, QCAA prescribed text lists and subject notices as the final authority where applicable.

Texts in contexts

Engage with a range of texts, including Australian texts, to explore production contexts, cultural identity, audience relationships, genre change and intertextual links.

Language and textual analysis

Analyse representations of concepts, identities, times and places; cultural assumptions, attitudes, values and beliefs; textual choices and audience positioning.

Responding to and creating texts

Create and analyse imaginative and analytical texts, synthesise evidence, experiment with form/style/tone and develop editorial independence.

Key vocabulary

cultural assumptionsIdeas a culture or group may treat as expected, normal or self-evident.
attitudesEvaluative stances or dispositions toward people, ideas or situations.
valuesPrinciples or qualities represented as important or desirable.
identityA textually constructed sense of self or group membership shaped by language, relationships and context.
adaptationA new text that reworks a source through changed medium, context, perspective or form.
reception contextThe circumstances and values of audiences encountering a text.

High-value revision routine

  1. Recall the concept or text evidence before opening notes.
  2. Answer the exact question in your own words.
  3. Reveal the checking guide and identify the missing reasoning.
  4. Revise the same response or retry a new A/B/C variant without copying a model.
