Unit 1 assessment context

For Unit 1, schools develop the assessment instrument or instruments used to judge achievement against the Unit 1 objectives/reporting standards. This app therefore practises transferable skills rather than claiming a single official Unit 1 instrument.

Useful preparation

  • Analyse evidence and draw conclusions.
