PAGE 4

Concept-card builder

Develop several distinct ideas with enough engineering detail to compare fairly.

A genuine range matters: avoid drawing one idea three times with only minor cosmetic changes. Each concept should represent a meaningful difference in mechanism, geometry, material, manufacture or operation.
PAGE 5

Weighted decision matrix

Use important success criteria to compare concepts, then justify the selection using engineering reasoning.

The matrix needs at least one success criterion and two developed concepts.

Weight each criterion from 1 (lower importance) to 5 (critical). Score each concept from 0 (does not satisfy) to 5 (strongly satisfies), and record evidence for the score. The highest total supports a decision, but does not replace the written justification.
