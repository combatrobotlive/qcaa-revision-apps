Student Experiment Companion

3. Repeated-measure uncertainty helper

Enter repeat measurements for one IV level. This calculates mean, half-range and half-range as a percentage of the mean — a common school-lab summary. Use the uncertainty method required by your teacher/instrument.

4. Linear trend helper

Enter comma-separated x and y values. The helper calculates a least-squares straight-line slope, intercept and R². A high R² does not by itself establish validity or causation.

5. Evaluation planner

A strong IA2 evaluation does not list generic improvements. Link the limitation → effect on evidence → specific improvement, then explain why the improvement helps.

UnitTopicsAssessment role

Summative internal assessment

  • IA1 Data Test — 10%: Unit 3; 5 min perusal + 60 min working; 2–4 unseen datasets; calculator + formula/data book.
  • IA2 Student Experiment — 20%: Unit 3; modify/refine/extend/redirect a class practical or simulation; up to 2000 words or 11 min multimodal.
  • IA3 Research Investigation — 20%: Unit 4; evaluate a teacher-provided claim using credible secondary evidence; up to 2000 words or 11 min multimodal.

External assessment — 50%

Two written papers covering science-understanding subject matter from Units 3 and 4. Each paper has 5 minutes perusal and 90 minutes working. Students may use an approved graphics/scientific calculator and the QCAA Physics formula and data book.
