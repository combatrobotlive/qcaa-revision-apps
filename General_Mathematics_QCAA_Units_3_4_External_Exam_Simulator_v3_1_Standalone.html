Units 3 + 4 external examination simulator

Original, seed-reproducible practice papers spanning both externally assessed units. This independent resource is not an official QCAA assessment paper.

Two papers · 95 marks

Verified assessment model

Paper 1 is simple familiar; Paper 2 is complex familiar and complex unfamiliar. Both model 5 minutes perusal and 90 minutes working, with a QCAA-approved scientific calculator and QCAA formula book.

Practice model
Paper 1 — 57 marks

15 multiple-choice questions (15 marks) and 10 short-response questions (42 marks).

Paper 2 — 38 marks

7 short-response questions. Guides reward working, mathematical reasoning and a contextual conclusion where required.

2026 scope and limitation

These are original practice questions based on the current Units 3 and 4 scope. The 2025 public paper counts are used as an authentic format model; QCAA’s 2026 guide cautions that past papers may not fully reflect revised subject matter or assessment specifications. Use the current syllabus, formula book and your teacher’s advice as authoritative.

Backup and migration safety

This simulator uses its own local storage namespace. It does not overwrite, merge or erase Unit 3 or Unit 4 progress. Export before changing devices or clearing browser data.

Use and review

Use with the current QCAA syllabus, formula book, approved calculator list and teacher direction. Schools should complete their own IT, accessibility, copyright and privacy review before deployment.

Privacy and analytics

The authorised GitHub Pages edition records one anonymous page visit using GoatCounter: page path, fixed app title and referrer origin only. It never sends answers, scores, local storage, school details or personal data. Downloaded, file://, local, custom-school and other-domain copies have no tracking.

Portability

Answers, timers, flags, results and branding remain in the browser. Downloaded copies are fully offline and tracker-free; downloading never embeds student progress or answers.
