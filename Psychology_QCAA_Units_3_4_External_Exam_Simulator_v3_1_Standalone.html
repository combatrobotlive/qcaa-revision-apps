Exam selection

The 2026 syllabus fixes two written papers, each with 5 min perusal + 90 min working. Exact 2026 raw marks and section counts are not specified in the syllabus, so this simulator uses the published 2025 49 + 46 raw-mark shape as a clearly labelled practice blueprint.

Permitted materials: a QCAA-approved graphics or scientific calculator. The Psychology syllabus does not specify a subject formula/data booklet for the EA. During timed perusal, questions and options are visible but responses are locked.
