LEGAL STUDIES v1

QCAA external-assessment practice

This app focuses on the current externally assessable content: Unit 4 Topic 1 — Human rights and Unit 4 Topic 3 — Human rights in Australian contexts.

360question variants
120question families
12generated legal cases
2025 EA reference: five short-response questions worth 19 marks and one 25-mark extended response to unseen stimulus (44 raw marks). Current 2026 conditions retain 15 minutes planning + 120 minutes working but do not specify an external-assessment word length.
Core exam habit: identify the legal issue → establish nature and scope → examine two viewpoints and consequences → integrate stimulus evidence → present two genuinely legal alternatives → justify a decision using legal criteria → discuss implications.
2026 additions: CRPD 2006, Convention against Torture 1984, Age Discrimination Act 2004 (Cth), and explicit analysis of Australian groups that protect or advocate for human rights.
