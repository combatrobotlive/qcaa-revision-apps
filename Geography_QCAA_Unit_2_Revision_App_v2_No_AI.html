Response toolkit

Build geographical reasoning

1. EvidenceSelect relevant values, locations, comparisons or observations.
2. PatternName the spatial distribution, trend, relationship or anomaly.
3. ProcessExplain the geographical or environmental process producing the pattern.
4. ChallengeInfer why the pattern creates a geographical issue for a specific place.
5. ActionGeneralise impacts and propose a feasible, evidence-based response.

Spatial and analytical language

Use words such as concentrated, dispersed, clustered, linear, gradient, core–periphery, upstream, downstream, exposure, vulnerability, capacity, interconnection, feedback where they accurately describe the evidence. Avoid treating a relationship as proof of one cause unless the evidence supports it.
