REFERENCE

Japanese EA guide

Current Unit 4 scope

Topic 1 — The present: end of school, graduation, technology/sustainability/innovation, social and global events, volunteering.

Topic 2 — Future choices: employment and second language benefits, post-school options and independence, Japanese in future study/travel/careers, global citizenship and maintaining relationships.

External assessment conditions

  • 5 minutes perusal, 120 minutes working.
  • Three to six Japanese stimuli, including written and audio/audiovisual material.
  • Combined Japanese stimulus length up to 1200 characters.
  • Questions may require English responses, Japanese short responses and extended Japanese responses.
  • No notes, dictionaries or reference materials.

2025 paper — useful practice pattern

  • Section 1: 15 marks, two short responses in Japanese, based on audio.
  • Section 2: 18 marks, five short responses in English, based on two written texts.
  • Section 3: 21 marks, one extended response in Japanese.

What the 2025 subject report says to strengthen

  • Context means the situation in which a text exists — not merely “letter”, “email” or “conversation”.
  • Tone should describe feelings/attitude, not “informative”.
  • Paraphrase stimulus evidence instead of copying long sections.
  • Use unique evidence; repeated information cannot keep earning evidence credit across questions on the same stimulus.
  • Extended responses should elaborate on all bullet points, use logical sequencing and synthesise ideas.
