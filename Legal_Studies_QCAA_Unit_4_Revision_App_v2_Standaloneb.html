Build confident legal language, then apply it to criminal investigation, trial and sentencing scenarios. This is focused Unit 4 learning and school-assessment revision, not an external-examination app.

Three modes per family: A builds the legal concept, B applies it to a short scenario, and C fixes a common misconception. Write an answer before opening the guide; then mark it honestly to make the filters useful.
Coverage: human rights; Australia’s legal response to international law and human rights; and human rights in Australian contexts. The bank is aligned to the QCAA Legal Studies 2025 v1.3 syllabus.

Assessment support

Unit 4 support: IA3 is an analytical essay using Topic 1 and Topic 2. The external examination relates to Topic 1 and Topic 3. Use your school task sheet, current sources and original writing for assessed work.

Use your teacher’s current task sheet and stimulus material for any assessed work. This app provides revision prompts and does not supply assessment responses.
