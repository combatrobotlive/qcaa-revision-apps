Maths entry: use * for multiplication, ^ for powers, and pi, sqrt(), ln(), exp(), sin() and cos(). Trigonometric expressions use radians. Use asin() and atan() for inverse trigonometry. Numeric checks accept fractions and scientific notation. Give at least four significant figures unless the question requests an exact value.

Expression checking samples numerical values; it is a practice aid, not a proof of algebraic equivalence. Written reasoning and working should be checked against the solution. A correct final answer alone does not demonstrate every available mark.
