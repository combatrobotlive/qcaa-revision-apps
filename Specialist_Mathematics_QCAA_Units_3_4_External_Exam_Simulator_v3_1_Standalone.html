Exam selection

The 2026 syllabus fixes Paper 1 technology-free and Paper 2 technology-active, each with 5 min perusal + 90 min working. It uses the published 2025 10 MC + 50 short-response marks per paper as a labelled practice blueprint.

Permitted materials: Paper 1: formula book only — no calculator, technology, notes or other resources. Paper 2: formula book plus a QCAA-approved non-CAS graphics calculator and/or QCAA-approved scientific calculator. During timed perusal, questions and options are visible but responses are locked.
