Scope: aligned to English General 2025 v1.3 (January 2026). This app is an independent revision resource, not a QCAA publication. Use the current syllabus, school task documents, QCAA prescribed text lists and subject notices as the final authority where applicable.

Texts in contexts

Explore how personal, social, historical and cultural contexts influence meaning; relationships between text, purpose, audience and context; genre, mode and intertextuality.

Language and textual analysis

Analyse perspectives and representations, audience positioning, cultural assumptions and values, language choices, argument, rhetoric, omission, inclusion and emphasis.

Responding to and creating texts

Develop evidence-based responses and create coherent texts using purposeful structure, cohesion, language, grammar and mode-appropriate features.

Key vocabulary

perspectiveA way of viewing or understanding ideas, people, events or experiences.
representationA constructed portrayal of a concept, identity, group, time or place.
positioningWays a text invites audiences to respond, think or feel.
contextPersonal, social, historical, cultural and other circumstances relevant to production or reception.
intertextualityMeaning created through relationships or references between texts.
synthesisCombining evidence or ideas to produce a stronger, integrated interpretation.

High-value revision routine

  1. Recall the concept or text evidence before opening notes.
  2. Answer the exact question in your own words.
  3. Reveal the checking guide and identify the missing reasoning.
  4. Revise the same response or retry a new A/B/C variant without copying a model.
