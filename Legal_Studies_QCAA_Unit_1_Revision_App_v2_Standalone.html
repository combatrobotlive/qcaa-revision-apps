Beyond reasonable doubt

Build confident legal language, then apply it to criminal investigation, trial and sentencing scenarios. This is focused Unit 1 learning and school-assessment revision, not an external-examination app.

Three modes per family: A builds the legal concept, B applies it to a short scenario, and C fixes a common misconception. Write an answer before opening the guide; then mark it honestly to make the filters useful.
Coverage: legal foundations; the criminal investigation process; criminal trial process; and punishment and sentencing. The bank is aligned to the QCAA Legal Studies 2025 v1.3 syllabus.

Assessment support

Units 1 and 2 use school-developed assessment. Practise the legal language, scenario application and evaluation chain required by your local task sheet.

Use your teacher’s current task sheet and stimulus material for any assessed work. This app provides revision prompts and does not supply assessment responses.
