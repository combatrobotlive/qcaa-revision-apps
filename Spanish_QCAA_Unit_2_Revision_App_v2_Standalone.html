Reference

Unit 2 language and response toolkit

Quick reminders for interpreting and creating Spanish in this unit.

Giving travel advice

Use deberías + infinitive, te recomiendo que + subjunctive, conviene + infinitive, hay que + infinitive and affirmative/negative commands.

Narrating experiences

Preterite advances completed events; imperfect gives background, repeated actions and descriptions. Use both deliberately.

Hypothetical evaluation

Si + imperfect subjunctive + conditional is useful for recommendations and hypothetical travel decisions.

Cultural precision

Use país, región, comunidad or grupo when evidence is specific. “Spanish-speaking” is broader than “Spanish” and does not imply one culture.
