UNIT 4 DATA PRACTICE

Short marine-data scenarios

All figures are simulated practice data. Use the prompts, then reveal an indicative answer.

Capture–recapture

Initially mark 100 fish. The second sample contains 150 fish, including 25 marked fish.

Try: Estimate population size using the Lincoln index.

Indicative answer

N = 100 × 150 / 25 = 600 fish.

CPUE comparison

Year 1: 600 t from 100 vessel-days. Year 2: 480 t from 120 vessel-days.

Try: Calculate percentage change in CPUE.

Indicative answer

CPUE changes from 6.0 to 4.0 t/vessel-day: −33.3%.

Quota allocation

A participant has 8% of a 1200 t total allowable catch.

Try: Calculate the quota.

Indicative answer

0.08 × 1200 = 96 t.

Aquaculture feed efficiency

A farm uses 1800 kg feed for 1100 kg net biomass gain.

Try: Calculate FCR and state one limitation of using it alone.

Indicative answer

FCR = 1.636. It omits issues such as energy, feed source, disease, escapes and pollution.

MeasureRelationshipUse carefully
