EXAM GUIDE

How to attack Engineering Studies

Current HSC specification: 100 marks, 3 hours plus 5 minutes reading time, 20 marks objective response and 80 marks short-answer. There are approximately seven Section II questions and about 25 items; at least two items are worth 6–8 marks.
1. Read the verb first

Outline gives the main features. Describe gives characteristics/features. Explain establishes cause and effect. Discuss considers issues and relationships, often positive and negative.

2. Engage the stimulus

NESA feedback repeatedly warns students not to ignore diagrams, data tables or the stated application. Use the actual material, load, circuit or drawing shown.

3. Mechanics: show the method

Draw the free-body diagram, state assumptions, resolve forces, use the correct lever arm/area, show units and then check whether the result is physically reasonable.

4. Materials: property → reason

Do not just list “strong, light, tough”. Name the correct material class/alloy, relevant property, manufacturing or heat-treatment process, and explain the effect in service.

5. Drawings are marks

Use drawing equipment when practising. Scale, projection, hidden detail, section conventions, true lengths and labels can carry several marks independently of written theory.

6. Expect a topic switch

Do not assume all parts of a Section II question belong to the same module. Read every part as a fresh engineering task.
