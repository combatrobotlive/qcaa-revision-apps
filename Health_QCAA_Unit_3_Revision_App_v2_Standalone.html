IA1 and IA2 support

Unit 3 contains IA1 Action research (25%) followed by IA2 Examination — extended response (25%) in the same elective topic.

IA1 · Action research

Individual written research report, up to 2000 words. Investigate a local/regional issue, complete context analysis and needs assessment, then develop a diffusion action strategy with a target group, methodology/resources, two diffusion process variables and RE-AIM-informed post-implementation tools.

IA1 · Suggested report features

Title page, table of contents, introduction, discussion, planning for action and reference list. Appendixes are optional. Keep analysis, interpretation and critique connected to the local evidence.

IA2 · Extended-response exam

Individual supervised response to unseen stimulus in an alternate community context: 15 minutes planning and 120 minutes working. Two approved pages of notes may be used. Evaluate the selected innovation with two RE-AIM steps, reflect using two diffusion variables, and justify one recommendation.

Quality checklist

Unit reference

Core language for accurate revision and assessment responses.

Social ecological model
A model of interacting individual, relationship, community and societal influences.
Innovation
An idea, practice, process, service or product perceived as new by a person, group or organisation.
Diffusion
The extent, pattern and rate at which an innovation spreads through a population.
Opinion leader
An influential person whose authority, status or credibility affects peers.
Champion/advocate
A well-connected person who facilitates and supports change.
Relative advantage
The degree to which an innovation is perceived as better than what it replaces.
Compatibility
How well an innovation fits values, needs, routines and the setting.
Trialability
The extent to which an innovation can be tested on a limited basis.
Observability
How visible the innovation or its results are to others.
Institutionalisation
The innovation becomes part of routine organisational practice and is maintained.

VerbWhat the response must do

CritiqueExamine information in detail through the relevant model to distinguish determinants.
SynthesiseCombine evidence into a new, coherent action strategy or decision.
EvaluateJudge methodology or resources against criteria such as RE-AIM.
ReflectAppraise impact or uptake and use the judgment to shape future action.
