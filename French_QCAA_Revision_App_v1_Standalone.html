CURRENT QCAA SCOPE

French external exam guide

2026 Unit 4 content

Topic 1 — The present

  • graduation and end-of-school celebrations in Australian and French-speaking communities
  • young people shaping the future through technology, sustainability and innovation
  • social and global events that interest young people
  • benefits and challenges of volunteering

Topic 2 — Future choices

  • employment prospects and benefits of speaking a second language
  • post-school options and independence
  • French in future study, travel and careers
  • global citizenship, future identity and staying connected with friends

External assessment conditions

  • Mode: written
  • Perusal: 5 minutes
  • Working: 120 minutes
  • No notes, dictionaries or reference materials
  • 3–6 unseen French stimulus texts
  • Combined French stimuli up to 1000 words
  • At least one written and one audio/audiovisual text

Possible response lengths

  • English response: up to 100 words per question
  • French short response: up to 100 words per question
  • French extended response: up to 400 words per question

The 2025 paper used 14 marks of French short response, 15 marks of English short response and a 21-mark extended French response, for 50 marks. This app uses that as a practice blueprint, not a promise of the 2026 question pattern.

What high-level responses do

  1. Answer the actual inference or conclusion first.
  2. Use the required number of unique details from the stimulus.
  3. Explain how each detail supports the conclusion, especially for tone, purpose and audience.
  4. In French responses, communicate meaning accurately with appropriate vocabulary, grammar, tense and register.
  5. In extended responses, address and elaborate on every task point, sequence ideas logically and use the conventions of the required text type.

Listening practice

The app's audio is generated by the browser from original French scripts. Voice quality depends on the device. Chrome/Edge usually selects an installed French voice. You can replay audio as often as needed, matching the 2025 paper instructions.

For a real mock exam, keep the transcript hidden until marking.

Useful self-check

  • Have I identified context beyond simply naming the text type?
  • Did I give the exact number of different examples requested?
  • Did I distinguish purpose from audience?
  • Did I describe tone and explain its effect?
  • Did I infer, not simply translate?
  • Is my French response within the word limit and in an appropriate register?
