Unit reference

Concise prompts for revision—not a replacement for the syllabus or class materials.

Formulation and reformulation

Combine or change food components, ratios, processes and structures for a defined nutrition consumer market. Test nutrition, palatability, functionality, safety and shelf life.

NPSC

Final score = baseline points − modifying points.

  • Category 1: score below 1
  • Category 2: score below 4
  • Category 3: score below 28

Passing the NPSC is one condition for a health claim; it does not approve every claim.

Label evidence

Use the nutrition information panel, realistic serving size, per-100 g or mL values, ingredients and applicable claim rules. Distinguish nutrient-content claims from health claims.

Nutrition consumer markets

Infant, toddler, adolescent, adult, pregnant, elderly, vegetarian or vegan, health-conscious, allergic or intolerant, obesity-related, type 2 diabetes, coronary heart disease and emerging markets.

Reformulation levers

  • Reduce salt, sugar, saturated fat, additives or chemicals.
  • Increase fibre or use suitable alternative components.
  • Test sensory, shelf-life and processing trade-offs.

Extended-response sequence

  1. Analyse the problem, stakeholder and data.
  2. Determine measurable success criteria.
  3. Synthesise evidence across alternatives.
  4. Evaluate each option against the criteria.
  5. Select, refine and justify recommendations with data.
