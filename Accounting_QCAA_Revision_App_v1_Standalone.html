SCOPE & METHOD

Accounting EA guide

Current syllabus: Accounting 2025 v1.4. The external assessment relates to Unit 4 Topic 2 and Unit 4 Topic 3. The syllabus allows multiple-choice, practical items and sentence/paragraph responses, with 15 minutes planning and 120 minutes working.

Topic 2 checklist

  • General journal and general ledger transactions.
  • Correction of errors and reconstruction of accounts.
  • Prepayments, accruals, unearned/accrued revenue and interest on overdue accounts.
  • Straight-line and diminishing-balance depreciation.
  • Provision for doubtful debts, bad debts and bad debts recovered.
  • Inventory discrepancies and lower of cost or net realisable value.
  • Closing and reversing entries; adjusted trial balance.
  • Fully classified Statement of Profit or Loss and Statement of Financial Position.
  • Fully classified Statement of Cash Flows with GST.

Topic 3 checklist

  • Profitability, liquidity and stability ratios.
  • Cash flow ratios.
  • Company-specific ratios: EBITDA, EPS, P/E and dividend yield.
  • Horizontal, vertical, trend and ratio analysis.
  • Industry benchmarking and limitations of comparison.
  • Interrelationship of the three financial statements.
  • Analysis of profitability, liquidity, stability and cash flows.
  • Evaluation using strengths and limitations.
  • Justified decisions and recommendations for future operations.

A repeatable practical method

  1. Identify what the transaction or adjustment changes.
  2. Separate GST where the question requires it.
  3. Decide the correct accounts and classifications.
  4. Record/process first; then prepare the report or ratio.
  5. Check debits = credits and Assets = Liabilities + Equity where applicable.
  6. Use the business goal to decide what the result means.

A repeatable analysis/evaluation method

  1. State the relevant result, ratio, trend or benchmark.
  2. Use specific financial data from the stimulus.
  3. Explain the relationship or issue — not just whether it rose or fell.
  4. Identify a strength and/or limitation relevant to the decision.
  5. Make a clear judgement against the goal or benchmark.
  6. Recommend a financially justified action.
Published 2025 paper: 10 multiple choice questions plus three short-response questions for 60 marks. QCAA’s 2025 subject report recommends stronger practice with the complete accounting process, unfamiliar/unstructured stimulus, choosing ratios from the correct performance area, and justifying evaluation with relevant financial evidence. The current syllabus does not prescribe that same raw-mark distribution for 2026.
