    REVISION APPROACH

    Use concepts with evidence

    Questions mix recognition, explanation, application, data analysis, strategy decisions and evaluation. Multiple-choice and numerical items are checked in the browser; written responses use an indicative self-check. Optional AI marking-advice exports are for completed practice only; no answers are sent automatically.

    NEW IN v2.2

    Marking advice, not replacement writing

    Export a completed exam attempt or your current revision answer as a prompt for a teacher-approved AI tool. Questions, stimulus data and indicative marking guidance travel with your response. Exam export stays locked during perusal and working time. Nothing is sent automatically, and AI suggestions never change saved marks.

    EXTERNAL-EXAM PRACTICE

    External-exam simulator

    Three original, fixed papers with stimulus charts and tables, a read-only perusal phase, a persistent timer and post-submission marking. Full simulation uses 5 minutes perusal and 120 minutes working. The 55-mark practice structure follows the 2025 paper, not a guaranteed 2026 blueprint.

These original papers use a 2025-style 55-mark practice blueprint: 10 multiple-choice marks, 28 short-response marks and 17 extended-response marks.       Full simulation has no pause. The timer continues while the tab is closed or in the background and resumes from the saved deadline. Answers and planning are locked during perusal; answer guides appear only after the attempt ends. Opening a blank paper before starting means it is no longer unseen to you.

      How to use this mode

      Choose an unused paper, close your notes and start the full simulation. Read during perusal, then answer all questions during working time. Use the numbered buttons to move around the paper and flag questions to revisit.

      After submission, multiple choice is checked locally. For written responses, compare your work with the indicative guidance and enter your own marks against each criterion. Those marks are not an official grade.

      Written exam, not an IA3 folio

      Use the data supplied in the paper to explain relationships, appraise a strategy and justify decisions. Do not import a personal folio or rely only on the physical activity studied in class. All sporting contexts are explained in the stimulus.

      Focused modes use suggested practice timings, not separate QCAA exam conditions. The printed papers support handwritten practice. After an attempt ends, the optional AI export prepares a marking-advice prompt for you to copy or download. There is no in-app AI marking or writing generator.

      Exam attempts on this browser

      Exam attempts use their own subject-and-unit storage key. Normal Unit 4 revision progress is unchanged. Storage belongs to this browser and site; moving devices or using private browsing can lose it. Back up important work.

      OPTIONAL / COMPLETED PRACTICE ONLY

      Export for AI marking advice

      Feedback, not rewriting - you remain the author.

      No automatic sharing. The app only prepares text on this device. You decide whether to paste it into a teacher-approved AI tool. AI advice can be wrong and is not an official result. Saved marks are never changed by this export.

      The reference guides are not your answers. Graphs are exported as labelled data, not screenshots. You may remove personal details from this export copy; your saved responses will not change. Keep the marking instructions, questions and criteria intact.

      Read and confirm the ethical-use statement to prepare the export.

      Paste the prompt into your approved AI tool yourself. The prompt asks for indicative marks, credit earned and specific improvement advice, not replacement writing. Follow your teacher's guidance if AI feedback and class feedback differ. These safeguards do not make this a secure invigilation system.
